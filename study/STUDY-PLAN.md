# BC Real Estate Trading Services Licensing Course — Study Plan

Source: UBC Sauder Real Estate Division, *Real Estate Trading Services Licensing Course Manual*, 2026 ed.
Full text extracted to `_text/` (ch01–ch26, preface, glossary) for fast search.
All 26 chapter PDFs present in `../Course material- Real estate trading services/` (same 2026 edition).

**Teaching mode: complete beginner.** No assumed background in law, finance, or real estate.
Every technical term gets defined in plain English before it is used. Heavy use of concrete scenarios.

## The course map — 26 chapters, 7 blocks

| Block | Chapters | Theme | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Legal foundation | Pref, 1, 3, 4 | Sources of law, courts, estates & interests, Torrens title | Heavy |
| B. Regulation & conduct | 2, 5, 9 | RESA, BCFSA, Rules, liability, FINTRAC, ethics | **Heaviest — exam favourite** |

## How each lesson runs
1. **Ground floor** — the everyday problem this chapter exists to solve, in plain language
2. **Vocabulary** — new terms defined before they are used
3. **Teach** — the substance, built concrete → abstract, with worked scenarios
4. **Trap list** — distinctions the exam tests and students get wrong
5. **Drill** — scaffolded questions, hints included
6. **Mindset note** — one habit of a top-tier agent, drawn from the chapter

## Standing rules
- Never memorize without a mechanism. If you can't say *why* the rule exists, you'll lose it under exam pressure.
- Every legal rule connects to a licensee failure mode. Learn the failure, not the rule.
- Numbers chapters need a calculator in hand (HP 10bII+). Do not read them passively.
