# Lesson 10 Cheat Sheet — Ch 6: Commercial and Residential Tenancies

## Two rulebooks
- **Commercial**: common law + **the lease** (+ *Commercial Tenancy Act*). **Residential**: *Residential Tenancy Act* (1984) + Regulation; **no contracting out**.
- First question: **residential or commercial?**

## Lease vs licence
- Lease = **interest in land**, runs with land, landlord-tenant relationship. Licence = personal permission; doesn't bind buyer; **no RTA/CTA protection**.
- Test (*Ocean Harvesters*, SCC 1974): **intention**; key = **exclusive possession**. Labels don't decide. Meals/housekeeper → licence; entry only to repair after notice → lease.

## RTA doesn't apply to
Business + dwelling under **one lease** · non-profit **co-op** members · **shared kitchen/bath with owner** · student residences, care facilities, shelters, jails · term **> 20 years** (→ commercial) · **vacation/travel**.

## Agreement contents
- Commercial: > **3 yrs** in writing (LEA s.59). Parties · premises · start · term (certain) · rent · material terms. Write everything (implied terms otherwise).
- Residential: writing, signed, dated, **8-pt**; s.13 terms (standard terms, names, address for service, start, periodic/fixed, rent + due date, services, deposits). Copy to tenant within **21 days**. Standard form optional.

## Tenancy types

| | Commercial | Residential |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed term ends | **Automatically**, no notice | → **month-to-month** (s.44(3)) unless valid vacate clause (sublease, or landlord/close family occupying, Reg 13.1) |
| Periodic created | Expressly, or **overholding + rent accepted** (yearly rent → yearly; monthly → monthly) | Expressly, or end of fixed term |
| Periodic ended | **Reasonable notice** to end of period: 1 period (weekly/monthly), **6 months** (yearly); *Union Properties*: 28 days OK; no form | Only Act grounds; prescribed forms |

- **Tenancy at will**: ends any time; seller lets buyer in early; regular rent → periodic. Not in RTA.

## Residential: landlord notices

| Ground | Notice | Dispute within |
|---|---|---|
| Non-payment (s.46) | ≥ **10 days**; void if paid within **5 days** | **5 days** |
| Cause (s.47) | ≥ **1 month**, end of period | **10 days** |
| Ceases to qualify, subsidized (s.49.1) | **2 months** | **15 days** |
| Landlord's/purchaser's use (s.49) | **3 months** | **21 days** |
| Demolition/conversion (s.49(6)) | **4 months** | **30 days** |
| Renovation (s.49.2) | **RTB order first**; ≥ 4 months after | hearing |

- Utilities unpaid **30 days** after written demand = rent. s.44.1: no frivolous notices (offence). s.56: early end in serious cases.
- Cause: deposit unpaid 30 days · repeatedly late · overcrowding · disturbance/jeopardy/risk · illegal activity · extraordinary damage · unrepaired damage · material term after written notice · assign/sublet w/o consent · **false info to buyers/tenants** · govt order · arbitrator order unmet 30 days.
- **Purchaser's use**: subjects **removed** · buyer an **individual** (or close family) occupying in good faith · **written request** → seller serves 3-Month Notice naming buyer.
- **Close family** = parent, spouse, child, spouse's parent/child.
- No landlord/purchaser use in **5+ rental-unit** non-strata (or single-owner) buildings. **Fixed term can't be cut short** for landlord use (can for cause/rent).
- Compensation: **1 month's rent** (withhold last month); **12 months** if purpose not done within reasonable time / not used **12 months**.
- Renovation (5+ units): **right of first refusal**; fail **45 days'** notice → 12 months.
- Tenant: ≥ **1 month** notice (periodic); after s.49/49.1/49.2 → **10 days'** notice.
- **Form (s.52)**: writing, signed, dated, address, effective date, grounds; landlord's wrong form = **void**.
- Discipline: vacant possession June 14 on May 7 contract → **7 days + $1,250 + course** (s.30, s.34).

## Deemed receipt
**Mail 5th day** · email **3rd** · fax **3rd** · door/mailbox **3rd**.

## RTB disputes
Courts excluded (except > Small Claims limit or linked to SC case). Copy of application within **3 days**. Arbitrator: flexible, any evidence, **not bound by precedent**, decision **30 days**, final, enforceable as SC order. Compliance unit (2019): up to **$5,000/day**. Manufactured homes: **MHPTA**, RTB disputes; $1,000 fines rules struck.

## Rights & obligations

| Issue | Commercial | Residential |
|---|---|---|
| Rent increases | per lease; renegotiate | **12 months** since last/move-in; **3 months'** notice, prescribed form; **CPI** cap (lower caps 2023–24); new tenant resets; new owner doesn't; additional increase via RTB (not "below market") |
| Deposit | negotiable; **personal** to landlord | max **½ month** (+ ½ month pet); interest; return **15 days** or **double**; **runs with land** (s.93) |
| Inspections | — | joint condition inspection; **2** opportunities; non-participation forfeits |
| Distress | **yes** | **abolished** (s.26(3)) |
| Mitigation | no duty; beware **surrender**; *Highway Properties*: terminate + sue with **notice** | **both must mitigate** (s.7(2)) |
| Quiet enjoyment | title + interference; lease can allow entry | **s.28** privacy, no unreasonable disturbance, exclusive possession, common areas (*Ashurwin* $700) |
| Entry | per lease | **s.29**: consent (≤ 30 days) or written notice **24 h–30 days**, **8 a.m.–9 p.m.**, reasonable purpose; emergency; abandoned; monthly inspection |
| Non-derogation | yes | yes |
| Interesse termini | **damages only** | abolished → order of possession |
| Repairs | **caveat lessee**; tenant-like manner; no waste; fair wear and tear | **landlord s.32** (even known defects, no contracting out); tenant cleanliness + own damage; emergency repairs: try contact **twice**, fix, deduct |
| Assign/sublet | free unless prohibited; read strictly; consent can cost | **written consent**; can't unreasonably refuse (**6+ month** fixed); **no fee** |

- **Gross** (all-in) → single net (taxes) → double net (+insurance) → **triple net** (+repairs; all costs). Read the lease.
- Commercial rent not due in advance by default; on time until **midnight**; Sunday OK. Remedies: sue/expedited CTA, **re-enter**, **distrain**.
- Sublease must keep a **reversion** (last day) or it's an assignment.

## Cannabis

## Licensee checklist
Fixed or periodic? Rent, deposits? Buyer occupying → 3-Month Notice + written request + end-of-period completion + compensation. Showings: s.29 notice, schedule with tenant, limit open houses, protect privacy in photos.
