# Lesson 7 Cheat Sheet — Ch 10: The Law of Contract

## Basics
- Contract = promise(s) courts enforce. Promisor / promisee; bilateral = mutual promises.
- Real estate deals: **agency contract** + **contract of purchase and sale**.
- **7 essentials**: Offer · Acceptance · Consideration · Legal intention · Capacity · Legal object · Genuine consent.
- Land contracts generally need **writing** (*LEA* **s.59**).

## Failed contracts

| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Void** | never existed (common mistake) |
| **Illegal** | void; court may refuse recovery |
| **Voidable** | valid until rescinded (minor, misrep, duress, undue influence) |
| **Unenforceable** | valid but procedural bar (oral land contract) |

## Offer
- Clear & unambiguous (two meanings → neither enforced). Specific offeree; **standing offers** to public.
- **Invitation to treat**: ads, listings, displays → not offers.
- Ends: time limit · reasonable time · **revocation** · death/incapacity · **counter-offer** · rejection.
- **Revocable before acceptance even if "open until…"**; must be **communicated** (*Byrne*). Two acceptances → **first in time** gets property; other gets damages.
- **Option** = separate consideration keeps offer open (land assembly). Lawyer review.
- **Counter-offer kills original**; inquiry doesn't. *Livingstone v. Evans*: "Will give $1,600" = counter; "Cannot reduce price" = renewal → accepted.
- Rules s.30: communicate all offers promptly.

## Acceptance
- Clear, positive act; **silence ≠ acceptance**. Use specified method, else same method.
- **Rules s.47**: copy of acceptance to **each party** + brokerage, promptly.

| Method | Acceptance effective |
|---|---|
| Instantaneous (phone, probably fax) | on **receipt** |
| Non-instantaneous (mail) | on **posting** (postal rule) |

- Slower method = offeree's risk. **Revocation: no postal rule**.

## Consideration
- Value given by each side; mutual promises suffice. **Adequacy not reviewed** ($1 ok) unless fraud etc.
- **Past consideration** ✗ (fence painting).
- **Seal** replaces consideration only if **knowingly** applied (*Pooni v. Storsley* 2022 → form now has **initial boxes**). Corporate seal ≠ consideration.
- Amendments: traditionally fresh consideration; *Rosas v. Toca* (2018 BCCA) may not need it; safest = **writing + $1 or seal**.
- **Quantum meruit**: reasonable pay when none fixed / partial performance / void contract / substituted. *Tapestry* (2009): no initials on 10% → **7%** (~$30K lost).

## Legal intention
Objective test. Commercial/strangers → **presumed yes**. Family/friends → **presumed no**. Rebuttable.

## Capacity
- **Minors (< 19)**: unenforceable **against** them, enforceable **by** them (*Infants Act* s.19). Enforceable if: statute · **affirm** after 19 · **perform** within 1 yr · **don't repudiate** within 1 yr.
- **Mental incapacity**: presumed capable; test = understand terms + effect + other party **knew**. *AGA* **s.60.2**: transfer stands if **full fair price** OR **no reasonable way to know**. (Dementia case: $1,000 + 30 days.)
- Steps: check POA/committee → legal advice → MB → if none, **ILA + certificate** → never accept POA yourself.

| Document | Key rule |
|---|---|
| POA | ends on death, incapacity, bankruptcy; land POA **3 yrs** (*LTA* s.56) |
| **Enduring POA** | survives incapacity; no 3-yr limit; may need trigger |
| **Committee** | court-appointed; adult **can't contract**; maybe PGT ($750 + 7 days case) |
| Representation agreement (≥ **Sept 1, 2011**) | **can't** sell real estate |
| Advance directive | health only |

- LTO rejects POA: name mismatch · aka names · wrong property/power · no out-of-province certificate · **expired** · donor dead/incapable · enduring not triggered.
- Illiterate/foreign/blind: bound if knew general nature; fraud → **non est factum**.
- **Corporations**: confirm existence; pre-incorporation contracts (BC BCA allows ratification; others differ) → legal advice.
- **Estate sales**: search for **joint tenant** · executor → **Grant of Probate** (3–6 mo; can list **subject to probate**) · administrator → **Letters of Administration** (no authority before) · sign "X, Executor of the Estate of Y".

## Legal object
Illegal contracts void; court may deny relief. *Shafazand* (2014): hidden illegal suite → **$42K** denied.

## Genuine consent
### Misrepresentation
False **fact** · induced · would induce reasonable person. Opinion ✗ unless **expert** (licensee = expert).

| Innocent | Fraudulent (knowing/reckless) |
|---|---|
| rescission **before completion** only; **no damages** | rescission **any time** + **damages** |

- **Entire agreement clause**: protects seller vs **innocent** only; **never** vs fraud; **never protects licensee**.
- *Betker v. Williams* (1991 BCCA): "building lot" not buildable → licensees negligent; seller vicariously liable.

### Defects
- **Caveat emptor**. **Patent** (visible/reasonable inspection) → buyer's problem. **Latent** → seller discloses only if **known** + **dangerous/uninhabitable**; no wilful blindness.
- *Gibb v. Sprague*: ants (unknown) ✗ · mould (known, hidden) ✓ liable · electrical (patent) ✗.
- Off-property dangers can count (landslide, radioactive soil).
- Advise **licensed inspector**; specialists for environmental; **document** if declined.
- **Rules s.59 material latent defect** (seller's licensee → all parties, **writing**, **before agreement**, **not in contract**): dangerous / unfit / unfit for known purpose · **great expense** · **local govt notice** · **no permits**. Broader than common law (illegal suite). Client says don't disclose → **stop acting**.
- **Oil tanks**: Fire Code; Vancouver: remove or permit after **2 yrs** unused; ask, then disclose.
- **Radon > 200 Bq/m³** = material latent defect (BCFSA); PDS asks.
- **Stigmas** (subjective): no duty to disclose; *Summach v. Allen* (nude beach). Asked → **truth or decline**. Buyers: ask, search, **warranty clause**.

### Mistake (fact, root of contract)

| Mistake | Result |
|---|---|
| Common (same) | **void** (destroyed cabin) |
| Mutual (different) | void if no reasonable reading (*Raffles*, two "Peerless" ships) |
| Unilateral (other knew) | court corrects (*Beverly Motel*: extra lot returned) |

**Non est factum** = "not my deed". Presumed to read what you sign.

### Duress · undue influence · unconscionability → **voidable**
Duress: physical or **economic**. Undue influence: dominant relationship. Unconscionable: **inequality + substantially unfair**; stronger party proves fairness.

## Termination
Performance (substantial counts) · agreement · **condition precedent unmet** · **frustration** · accepted **breach**. Plus statutory: REDMA 7-day; **HBRP** 3 business days, 0.25%.

### Conditions precedent
"Subject to" event (not time). Seller → backup offers only. **Waiver (*LEA* s.54)**: **sole benefit** · performable without it · **before deadline**. Both benefit → both consent. Write **"for the sole benefit of the [Buyer/Seller]"**.

### Frustration
Outside event **after** contract; fundamentally different; not provided for (fire; rezoning — *Capital Quality Homes*, deposit returned). Can't self-frustrate. **Common mistake = before; frustration = after.**

### Breach
Anticipatory (sue now or wait) · make impossible · fail on date.

| Breach of | Innocent party's options |
|---|---|
| Condition (fundamental; "**time is of the essence**") | end + damages · continue + damages · continue + **specific performance** |
| Warranty (minor) | **damages only**; still perform |

- *Gill v. Bal* (2017): 4 p.m. deadline missed (5:36) → buyer breach, contract ended.
- *Bhasin v. Hrynew* (SCC 2014): **honest performance** duty.
- **Rules s.48**: never induce breach to switch deals.

## Assignment
- **Privity**: only parties sue (exceptions: runs with land, assignment).
- Assign **benefits**, not burdens. Assignee consents; original promisor's consent not needed.
- **Statutory (*LEA* s.36)**: **writing** · **absolute & unconditional** · **written notice** to promisor. Else equitable (all 3 parties in suit).
- **Regulation s.8.2** standard terms in licensee-drafted offers: no assignment without **seller's written consent**; **seller gets profit**. Excluded → **prescribed notice** to seller's side. (Not REDMA.)
- **Vicarious performance** ≠ assignment (original party stays liable); not for **personal** contracts.

## Remedies

| Remedy | Notes |
|---|---|
| Damages | **common law**, as of right; *Hadley v. Baxendale* (natural/foreseeable); **mitigate**; liquidated = genuine pre-estimate else **penalty** |
| Specific performance | **equitable**, discretionary; damages inadequate; property must be **unique** (not automatic now; *Sihota v. Soo* granted) |
| Injunction | equitable; prohibit or **mandatory**; contempt |
| Quantum meruit | reasonable value (*Banfield v. Hoffer* commission; proof is hard) |
