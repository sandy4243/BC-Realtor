# Lesson 14 Cheat Sheet — Ch 18–19: Local Government Law and Property Taxes

## Who regulates land
- **Federal**: only through its own powers (airports, fisheries); federal land not zoned.
- **Provincial**: **ALR** (~5M ha; ALC records beat title; new house ≤ **500 m²**) · septic (*Public Health Act*) · **Islands Trust** · rezoning within **800 m** of a controlled access highway intersection · *EMA* site disclosure statement (Schedule 2 uses) · heritage (undesignated sites too: *Mackay*).
- **Local**: *Community Charter* (municipalities) · *Local Government Act* (regional districts + planning) · *Vancouver Charter*. Creatures of the province.
- Regional district (board; acts as municipality in unorganized areas) · municipality (mayor + **4–8** councillors, **4 yrs**) · improvement district (trustees; services only, **no** planning).

## Why regulate
Health and safety · property values · efficiency/convenience/appearance · conserving resources.

## OCP vs zoning

| | OCP | Zoning |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | Strategic, optional | Detailed rules |
| Time | Current + **future** | **Current** only |
| Effect | Later bylaws must be **consistent** | Can't conflict with OCP |

- OCP housing policy ≥ **5 yrs**; update every **5 yrs**; public hearing; not a commitment to spend.
- Zoning: map + general sections + zone rules. Controls buildings per lot, setbacks, height, **FSR** (floor area ÷ lot area), home occupations, parking/loading, signs. **CD** = site-specific.
- Infill: strata-titled coach house can be sold separately; laneway on same lot can't.
- **Rezoning**: 3 readings + final adoption; public hearing if **inconsistent** with OCP (none for OCP-consistent residential); conditions/letter of credit; **no compensation**.
- **Land use contracts** (1971–78): all ended **June 30, 2024**.
- **STRAA** (2023): principal residence + 1 suite/ADU; fines to **$50,000** (RD), tickets **$3,000/day**; local rules can be stricter. *Whistler*: rental pool bylaw valid.
- Illegal suite: don't advertise as income (**s.41**); disclose as material latent defect (**s.59**); buyer's agent checks (**s.30(h)**).

## Non-conforming use
- Continues through sales · lost after **6 months** unused (**90 days** Van) · no structural change unless law/BoV · spreads in building, not onto more land · **75%+** damage → rebuild to current rules.

## Variances and permits

| | Who | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| **Board of variance** | Appointed (mandatory if zoning) | **Minor**, **undue hardship**, siting/size/dimensions |
| **DVP** | Council | Many bylaws; **not use or density** |
| **DP** | Staff/council | Only in OCP-designated **DP areas**; often before building permit |
| **TUP** | Council | Temporary commercial/industrial; ≤ **3 yrs**; renew **once** |

## Subdivision and building
- **Approving officer** can refuse for **public interest**; court review only for bad faith, discrimination, specious basis (*Hlynsky*).
- Developer pays servicing; parks ≤ **5%**. **DCCs** = bylaw; **CACs** = negotiated, not compulsory.
- Building permit **must** issue if compliant. Complex buildings: registered professionals certify. **s.57** notice = contravention (check city records anyway).
- **Licensing bylaws** can block a use zoning allows (pet store).

## GST (5%, *Excise Tax Act*)
- **Taxable unless exempt** (Schedule V). Exempt: sale by a **non-builder**, long-term rentals, some personal-use land.
- Buyer **pays**; seller **collects** (liable if not). **Self-assess**: GST-registered buyer (not individual + residential complex) or **non-resident** seller.
- No GST on deposit until seller's. ITCs (business) · New Housing Rebate (apply ≤ **2 yrs**). Objection **90 days**; Tax Court **90 days**.
- **Builder** = interest during construction/substantial renovation or before first occupancy (individuals: profit motive). **Self-supply**: builder moves in or rents → GST then.
- Short-term rentals → **hotel exclusion**. Excess land assessed separately.
- CPS since **Nov 2024**: price **includes GST if applicable**; silent old contracts → GST **extra**.

## PTT (buyer pays at registration; FMV incl. fixtures)

| Portion | Rate |
|---|---|
| ≤ $200K | **1%** |
| $200K–$2M | **2%** |
| > $2M | **3%** |
| Residential > $3M | **+2%** |

- $1.5M = **$28,000** · $3M = **$68,000** · $4M residential = **$118,000**.
- **First-time buyer**: FMV ≤ **$835K**, ≤ **0.5 ha**, never owned → no PTT on first **$500K** (partial to $860K). **Newly built**: ≤ **$1.1M** (partial to $1.15M).
- Presale **strata** assignments: price + assignment amounts. Non-strata presales: FMV at closing. *Zellstoff Celgar*: fixtures → **$4.5M**.
- **Foreign buyers' tax 20%**: residential in Metro Van, CRD, Fraser Valley, Central Okanagan, Nanaimo RD. One foreign co-buyer taints all. PNP exemption; rebate if PR/citizen within **1 yr**. *Shave*: licensee **75%** liable.
- Federal non-Canadian purchase ban until **Jan 1, 2027**.

## Selling taxes
- Non-resident seller: withhold **25%** (non-depreciable) / **50%** (depreciable) of **gross** price unless clearance certificate; buyer protected by **reasonable inquiry** (*Mao v. Liu*).
- Capital gain **50%** taxable · business income **100%** · principal residence exempt. *Happy Valley Farms*: motive; **secondary intention**. *Wall*: flips = business income.
- Federal flipping: < **365 days** → business income (life-event exceptions). BC flipping tax: < **730 days**, **20%** in year 1, own return ≤ **90 days**.

## Property tax
- Tax = net taxable value ÷ 1,000 × **mill rate**. Owner on title **Nov 30** liable (incl. life tenant, agreement for sale holder).
- **Actual value** = market value at **July 1** (condition/use as of **Oct 31**). Roll done **Dec 31**.
- Classes: 1 residential · 2 utilities · 3 supportive housing · 4 major industry · 5 light industry · **6 business** · 7 managed forest · 8 rec/non-profit · 9 farm. **Split classification** for mixed use.
- Additional school tax: **0.2%** $3–4M, **0.4%** > $4M.
- Appeals: complaint by **Jan 31** → PARP (**Feb 1–Mar 15**) → roll final **Mar 31** → PAAB by **Apr 30** → BCSC (law). Appeal the **assessment**, not the tax.
- Relief: farm value · **10-yr** resident on up-zoned land · golf/cemetery agreements (≥10 yrs).
- **Home owner grant** $570 (Metro Van, FV, CRD) / $770 (north/rural); pay ≥ **$350**; more for seniors 65+, veterans, disability.
- **Deferment**: 55+/spouse/disability with **25%** equity; families with children **15%**; registered charge; repay before sale.

## Vacancy taxes

| | UHT | SVT | EHT |
|---|---|---|---|
| Level | Federal | BC | Vancouver |
| Rate | **1%** | **3%** foreign/satellite; **1%** Canadians | **3%** |
| Filing | Return by **Apr 30** (penalty ≥ $1K/$2K) | Declaration by **Mar 31** | PSD (fine ≤ $10K if false) |
| Occupied test | **180 days** | rented **6 months** (1-month+ periods) | **6 months**; rentals **30+ days** |

- SVT BC-resident credit **$4,000** (2026+) → no tax up to **$400K**. No declaration = max rate.
