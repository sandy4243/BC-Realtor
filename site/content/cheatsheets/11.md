# Lesson 11 Cheat Sheet — Ch 7: Strata Properties and Cooperatives

## Basics
- Strata law since **1966**; *Strata Property Act* from **July 1, 2000**; Standard Bylaws for all from **Jan 1, 2002**.
- Strata lot = fee simple; **common property** = tenants in common (incl. pipes/wires in boundaries or serving others).
- **Unit entitlement** (Form V): share of CP **and** expenses. Lot share = UE ÷ total UE × expense. Residential = **habitable area**. Other formula = **unanimous** + registered. Check **General Index** (myLTSA).
- Pre-2000 plans: **Schedule of Interest on Destruction**.

## Exclusive use of CP

| | LCP | Short term exclusive use |
|---|---|---|
| Attaches to | **lot** (runs with land) | person |
| Created | developer on plan · **unanimous** plan amendment · **¾** resolution filed (Common Property Record) | **council majority**; significant change **¾** (majority if EV) |
| Term | until removed | ≤ **1 yr** (EV stall **5 yrs**); cancellable |

- Parking/lockers: separate lot (old only) · part of lot · LCP · CP licence/lease (strata or developer).
- **EV**: owner request (s.90.1); decide within **3 months**; can't unreasonably refuse; ¾ approvals → **majority**.

## Strata corporation
- Created on **deposit** of plan: "The Owners, Strata Plan ___". Capacity of natural person.
- **Judgment vs strata = vs every owner** (s.166) by UE (not the suing owner).
- **Sections** (s.191): residential/non-residential, townhouse/apartment; own executive; own costs.
- Duties: **manage · repair/maintain · insure** (full replacement + liability) **· enforce · keep records**. Buyers need own insurance (contents, deductibles).
- Records (s.35/36): owners, tenants with assigned rights, written authorizees. Copies **2 weeks** (bylaws/rules **1 week**). Tenant **3+ yr** lease auto-assigned (s.148, notice); else s.147 written notice.

## Buyer's information

| Doc | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **Form B** (s.59) | fees · owed amounts · alteration agreements · approved levies · budget overruns · **CRF** · unfiled bylaws/resolutions · upcoming ¾/80%/unanimous notices · **lawsuits** · work orders · parking/storage · insurance summary · electrical report; + rules, budget, **depreciation report**. **8 days, $35** + 25¢/page |
| **Form F** (s.115) | money owing (fees, levies, fines, judgment share, remedy costs; not unproven). **1 week, $15**, valid **60 days**; needed to register (not court sales); lawyer orders |
| Seller's authorization | no inspection fee; **25¢/page** |

- *Sask v. Brooke*: minutes showed leaks → **no reasonable reliance** ($60K levy; $133K → $22K).
- Review **2 years**: council, AGM/SGM, section minutes; budget, financials; bylaws, rules.
- 2019 discipline: missed roof project, subject-free → **$17,402** levy → reprimand.

## Governance
- **Council** (s.26): exercises powers, enforces bylaws. s.31: honest, good faith, **reasonably prudent person**. Std Bylaw 22: no personal liability if honest.
- Developer = first council (s.6). **First AGM** within **6 weeks** of earlier of **50%+1** conveyed or **9 months** after first conveyance. Late: **$1,000** first 30 days, then **$1,000/7 days**.
- **20 days'** notice; AGM within **2 months** of year-end; SGM on **20%** demand; electronic OK.
- Co-owners split → vote not counted. Mortgagee: right in mortgage + **3 days'** notice; insurance/maintenance/finance/security only. Age **16**.
- Strata managers: **licensed**; act for corporation; instructions from **council**; fiduciary.

| Vote | Uses |
|---|---|
| Majority | budget · ratify rules · EV · CRF for depreciation report/its items/electrical report · hire brokerage (*Dubas*) |
| **¾** | bylaws (after 2nd AGM) · levy by UE · CRF spending · LCP resolution · significant CP change · off-budget operating spend |
| **80%** | wind up (+ **court order**) |
| Unanimous | strata plan amendment · levy not by UE · new expense formula · residential bylaws before 2nd AGM |

## Finances
- **Operating** (≥ once/yr) vs **CRF** (< once/yr). CRF min **10%** of operating budget. **No CRF refund** on sale (s.101).
- Operating spend: budget · ¾ · council limit (default lesser of **5% / $2,000**) · emergency.
- CRF spend: ¾ (majority for depreciation/EV/electrical) · emergency incl. insurance; tell owners.
- **Interim budget**: from first conveyance to first AGM; given to buyers; developer pays unsold lots. Shortfall → developer pays; > **10%** → **double/triple**. Surplus refunds ≤ **$100** → CRF.
- **CRF seed**: lesser of 10% × years since deposit, or **50%** (within 1 yr = 10%). From **July 1, 2027** (5+ lots): first depreciation report funding = lesser of **$30,000** or **$5,000 + $200/lot**.
- **Special levy**: **¾** by UE / **unanimous** otherwise; state purpose, amounts, dates; safety repair fails ¾ → majority + court within **90 days**; surplus to current owners (≤ $100 → CRF). **s.109**: owner when due pays. Holdback: **s.108** wording ("approved", "special levy").
- **Depreciation report** (mandatory Dec 2011): qualified person, on-site, **30-yr** forecast; every **5 yrs**; first: **18 months** (formed ≥ Jul 1 2027) / **2 yrs** (Jul 2024–Jun 2027); exempt **< 5 lots**.
- **Electrical planning report** (5+ lots): **Dec 31 2026** Metro Van/Fraser Valley/CRD; **Dec 31 2028** elsewhere; new = 5 yrs. Attach to Form B.
- Collecting: **2 weeks'** demand. **Lien**: fees, levies, work costs, judgment share, filing costs; **not fines**. Fines → sue (2-yr limit) or **withhold Form F**. Lien beats mortgages; not builders liens, Crown, judgment-share liens. Forced sale → vesting order **free and clear**.

## Bylaws & rules

| | Bylaws | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| By | owners | council |
| Covers | lots, CP, admin | **CP/assets only** |
| Effective | **filed at LTO** | **majority ratification** at next AGM |

- Illegal (Act, *Human Rights Code*) = unenforceable. Can't restrict sale/lease/mortgage (except 55+).

| Strata type | Before 2nd AGM | After |
|---|---|---|
| Residential | unanimous | **¾** |
| Non-residential | ¾ / bylaws | ¾ / bylaws |
| Mixed | unanimous | ¾ **each** group |

- Enforce: complaint → written particulars → chance to answer/**hearing** (notify landlord) → written decision.
- Fines: Standard **$50** bylaw / **$10** rule; max **$200 / $50** every **7 days**; short-term rental **$1,000/day**. Remedy work. Deny rec facility. Landlord **vicariously liable**.
- *Bahmutsky v. Petkau* (CRT 2020): smoking nuisance; failed enforcement → strata paid half of **$1,000**.
- **Rentals**: no restrictions since **Nov 24, 2022** (short-term bans OK). **Form K** + bylaws to tenant; to strata within **2 weeks**; breach → tenant may leave within **90 days**, moving costs ≤ 1 month's rent.
- **Age**: **55+** only; grandfathered residents, caregivers, children/younger spouses. *Stevens*: $200/week, **$13,000+**. Discipline: outdated bylaws → **14 days** + 2 courses.
- **Pets** (std): fish, small caged mammals, **2 birds**, **1 dog or 1 cat**; existing pets grandfathered; **guide/service dogs** always (s.123(3)).
- *Kunzler* (2020/2021): cannabis bylaw passed after buyer disclosed plan → **not significantly unfair**.

## Repairs
Strata: **CP** (s.72); LCP **< once/yr**; always **structure, exterior, balconies, exterior doors/windows/skylights, fences/railings**. Owner: rest of lot + routine LCP. Pay by UE generally. Alterations: consent + **indemnity** binds buyers.

## Wind-up & lawsuits
- **80%** + court (ss.272–289); hire brokerage = **majority** (*Dubas* 2019 BCCA).
- s.165 injunction (owner, tenant, mortgagee, interested person); s.164 **significantly unfair** (incl. **50%+** voter). CRT, arbitration, administrator.

## Leasehold strata (Part 12)
- Leasehold landlords: BC/Canada, municipality, regional district, Nisga'a, treaty FNs (Tsawwassen, Maa-nulth, Tla'amin), public authorities (university, school board, Sechelt, PRHC…).
- **Ground lease ≥ 50 yrs** → strata lot leases → **assigned** to buyers (leasehold tenants). **Schedule of Restrictions**.
- Fees + levies + taxes + possible **rent** (reviews). Wasting asset.
- Expiry: **1 yr** notice; none → **auto 5-yr** renewal; renewal ≥ **5 yrs**; non-renewal → landlord **buys** (lease formula / schedule / FMV; arbitration). Vancouver says **land excluded** (unsettled). Convert to freehold (s.216, all lots).
- **No HBRP**. Search title, get lease, price for remaining term, legal advice, lender requirements; decline if no expertise; s.30(d).

## Co-ops

| | Strata | Co-op |
|---|---|---|
| Created | strata plan | incorporation (*Cooperative Association Act*) |
| Owner holds | fee simple + CP share | **shares** + long **lease** |
| Selling | transfer; easy mortgage | shares + lease; **board approval**; harder financing (PPSA) |

- Shares by value (2/50 = 4%). Blanket mortgage risk if members default. REDMA disclosure applies. **HBRP applies** to co-op interest with right to occupy.
