# Lesson 13 Cheat Sheet — Ch 15–17: Mortgage Law, Analysis and Underwriting

## The mortgage
- Interest in land as **security** + evidence of debt. Mortgagor = **borrower**; mortgagee = **lender**. BC: treated as a **transfer**; borrower stays registered owner.
- **Equity of redemption**: equity's right to redeem after default → ended by **foreclosure**. ≠ "equity" (value).
- **Legal** = registered (binds all). **Equitable** = binds borrower only: deposit of duplicate certificate (**intent**; no caveat) · agreement to mortgage (caveat) · disguised transfer.

## Implied terms
- **No clog**: lender's option to buy (with loan) **void**; 100-yr redemption void. Prepayment limits / no-assumption **OK**.
- Collateral advantage: void if a mortgage term that **outlasts redemption**.
- *Bhasin* (SCC 2014) honest performance; *Pastula* (2016) no new contract.

## Form B clauses
- Filed standard terms → borrower gets **copy + acknowledges**, or **prescribed** terms apply.
- **Acceleration**: lender's option; *LEA* s.25 relief, **once** (s.28). **Omnibus**: lender pays, adds to mortgage. **Advances**: no obligation. **Due on sale**: none → **assumable**. **Portability**: ~60 days, blended rate. Strata: vote assigned.
- **Covenantor** = primary; **guarantor** = secondary.
- Special: **interim blanket** (partial discharges) · **VTB** · **RAM** · **bridge**.

## Interest Act and Criminal Code

| | Rule |
|---|---|
| s.3 | No rate stated → **5%** |
| s.6 | Blended → yearly/half-yearly statement, else **no interest** |
| s.7 | Lower stated rate wins |
| s.8 | No higher rate on arrears (*Krayzel* SCC 2016); 3 months' interest on default void |
| s.10 | **Individual**, term > 5 yrs, after **5 yrs**, + **3 months' interest**; not corps; not partnerships/ULCs/trusts since **2012**; re-dated renewal restarts clock |

- Bonus = **principal** (Interest Act) but **interest** (Criminal Code).
- Criminal rate: **APR > 35%** from **Jan 1, 2025**; non-individual business loans: $10K–$500K ≤ **48%**; > $500K none.
- *R. v. Frebold* (2001): licensee POA fraud, **30 months**.

## Provincial
- **BPCPA** unconscionable: pressure, infirmity/ignorance, price out of line, no ability to pay, harsh terms. *Canmerica* (2015): inequality + unfairness → lender proves fairness. Court can rewrite.
- Part 5 disclosure (personal loans): **2 days** before · yearly (floating) · **30 days** after 1%+ rise/amendment · **21 days** before renewal · monthly (open).
- *Mortgage Brokers Act*: **$1,000+** fees or **10+** loans/yr → register.
- *EMA*: lender liable only if it causes contamination or becomes **owner** (order absolute).

## Assignment / assumption
- **Assignment**: no consent; **notice** freezes accounts; actual balance + set-off; estoppel.
- **Assumption**: seller **stays liable**; lender can sue buyer **directly** (PLA).
- PLA release (**residential purpose**): written approval (request ≤ **3 months**, not unreasonably refused) · no demand ≤ **3 months** after maturity · demand loan: no demand ≤ 3 months of notice.
- **Novation**: full liability + principal debtor + full satisfaction.

## Priorities
- **Registration order** (unless "subject to" / priority agreement). **Redeem up, foreclose down.**
- Further advances keep priority: **no written notice** · **obliged** · **priority agreement**.
- Statutory: builders lien (after filing) · wage certificate · **taxes** (no registration; tax sale **3 yrs**/2 rural; **1-yr** redemption) · **strata lien** · WorkSafeBC · **CRA deemed trusts** (no registry).

## Foreclosure
1. Demand (+ *BIA* s.244 **10 days**; farmer **15 business days**). 2. **Petition** (BC Supreme Court) + affidavit + CPL. 3. Respondents: later charges + **tenants** (for vacant possession).

| Order | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Order nisi** | Redemption period (~**6 months**; shorter if no equity); owner **can sell** |
| **Conduct of sale** | Lender lists; owner's listing **ends**; court approval needed |
| **Approving sale** | Market value check; **sealed higher bid**; title free and clear |
| **Order absolute** | Lender owns; keeps surplus; **loses covenant** (PLA s.32) |

- Proceeds: taxes/utilities → strata fees → commission → lender + costs → **into court**. Judicial sale → borrower liable for deficiency.
- Buyer: find **court dates** yourself; **as is, where is**; insure when unconditional.

## Agreement for sale
- Seller **stays on title**; buyer's interest = charge (**Form C**, "right to purchase"). Financially = VTB. Remedies: order nisi (~6 months), cancellation/forfeiture or specific performance, judicial sale.

## Financing and value
- **Cash-equivalent = cash down + PV of mortgage at market rate.**
- Contract rate below market → offer worth **less**; above market → worth **more**.
- Steps: contract PMT (+ OSB at term) → market rate, N = payments to receive → PV → + cash.
- Shorter term → less giveaway. Assumption: N = **remaining** payments.
- Example: $300K + $600K VTB at j₂ 3% vs 6% market → **$743,801** (fully amortized), **$853,446** (3-yr term).

## Fees and penalties
- Discount (deducted) / bonus (added): borrower pays on more than received.
- **APR** disclosure (BPCPA). **COFA**: PMT from **gross**, PV = **net**, N = **term** → shorter term = **higher** COFA.
- Lender pays broker → **lender yield ↓**. Buying a loan at a discount → yield ↑.
- Prepayment penalty: greater of **3 months' interest** or **IRD**. Open / closed / partially open (privileges).

## Residential underwriting
- **5 Cs**: character · capital · capacity · credit · collateral. Pre-approval holds rate **60–120 days**.
- Prime / Alt-A / subprime. Scores **300–900**; **750+** best; CMHC **680**. R1–R9, I1–I9.
- Self-employed: **35%** down (OSFI). Insured rental income 100% (not illegal suites, CMHC); conventional offset ≤ 90%.
- Price < cost; **lending value ≤ market value**; use the **lower**.
- **Max loan = LTV × lending value**: conventional **80%**, insured **95%**, private 75–90%.
- **GDSR** = (P+I+T) ÷ GI; **TDSR** = all debts ÷ GI; insured max **39% / 44%**. Lend the **lowest**.
- **Stress test** (federally regulated): greater of **5.25%** or **contract + 2%**.
- More loan: longer amortization · shorter term/lower rate · second mortgage (combined LTV).
- CMHC premium: ≤65% **0.60** · ≤75% **1.70** · ≤80% **2.40** · ≤85% **2.80** · ≤90% **3.10** · ≤95% **4.00** (4.50 non-traditional). Not in LTV; **in GDSR**.
- Min down: **5%** to $500K + **10%** to $1.5M; **$1.5M+ = 20%**, uninsurable.
- Insure for **full insurable value**; lender tax accounts; interest adjustment.

## Commercial underwriting
- Income property → **NOI** (after vacancy, bad debt, operating costs; before debt, tax, CCA).
- LTV **60–80%**. **DCR = NOI ÷ annual debt service** → max debt service = NOI ÷ DCR.
- DCR = **1 ÷ (1 − safety margin)**: 20% → **1.25**; 25% → 1.33.
- Apartments ~**1.2** (1.3–1.35 volatile); industrial **1.4–1.5**.
- Example: NOI $240K, DCR 1.25, j₂ 6%, 25 yr → **$2.50M** vs LTV 65% of $4M = $2.6M → DCR binds.
