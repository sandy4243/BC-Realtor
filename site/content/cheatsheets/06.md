# Lesson 6 Cheat Sheet — Ch 5 & Ch 9: Liability, FINTRAC and Ethics

## Where liability comes from
**Courts** (lawsuits) · **BCFSA** (≤ $250K, suspension, lose commission) · **Boards** (REALTOR® Code: reprimand → **expel**; no MLS®; **can't touch licence**) · **FINTRAC**.
Top 4 grounds: **negligence** · **misrepresentation (most common)** · **drafting errors** · **breach of fiduciary duty**. Standard ≈ close to perfection.

## Negligence — prove ALL 3
1 **Duty** (foreseeable + proximity) · 2 **Breach** (competent licensee standard; higher risk = higher standard) · 3 **Damage not too remote** (foreseeable).
- Examples: value advice · no subject-to-financing · water supply · buyer's ability to pay · boundaries · unverified listing info · no title search · misleading rent estimates.
- *Koerber* (2018): **general** disclaimer ("not guaranteed") failed → use **specific** ones. **No** disclaimer protects known falsehoods.
- Buyer's agent can't verify → send to experts / **subject clause** shifting risk.
- *Shaw v. Thody*: landslide risk; dismissed (expert evidence weak) → ask experts.
- **Vicarious liability**: brokerage liable for acts in **ordinary course** (even fraud); reps treated as employees.

## Misrepresentation

| Negligent | Deceit / fraudulent |
|---|---|
| careless, no intent | **knowing or reckless** |
| skilled person (special relationship) | **anyone** |

**Cognos 5**: special relationship · untrue/misleading · negligent · **reasonable reliance** · damage.
Expert's 3 options: refuse · advise **with disclaimer** · advise without (full responsibility).
- *Holt v. Thompson*: "no leaks" condo → liable.
- Second-hand info → **in writing with source**. PDS isn't armour. Liable to seller too for non-disclosure.
- Not structural experts (*Arthur v. Greyfriars*).
- **Rules ss.49–50**: inducement promises → **signed written statement, all details** ($500 inspection promise case).
- Clients talking directly to other side → warn them.

## Drafting & fiduciary
- *Russell v. Wispinski*: vague amendment = **counter-offer** → deal died → **negligent**.
- Fiduciary: **loyalty** + conflicts · disclosure · confidentiality · lawful instructions. **Burden on agent** to prove compliance.
- *Ocean City Realty* (BCCA 1987): secret commission split with buyer → **whole commission forfeited**.

## Money laundering
- Criminal Code **s.462.31**; knowing, believing or **reckless**. Mostly **not cash**.
- BC 2018: **$7.4B** (Canada $46.7B); ~**5%** price effect; "**Vancouver Model**".
- Why RE: high value · stable · simple · appreciates · many methods · **subjective value** · weak oversight · **anonymity** · flipping normal · hot markets.
- **Stages**: **Placement** (smurfing, casinos) → **Layering** (complex transactions) → **Integration** (renos then sale).
- Schemes: mortgage paydowns / quick discharges / **loan-back** / unregulated lending / fake income · rentals · renos · criminal ops · over/under valuation · no financing · **aborted purchases** · **HBRP abuse** · **straw buyers** · rapid flips.

## PCMLTFA / FINTRAC
- **FATF** 1989 (+terrorist financing after 9/11). PCMLTFA 4 objectives.
- **Reporting entities**: licensees **acting in purchase/sale** (not pure rental/strata mgmt), developers, MBs, accountants, notaries, casinos…
- **FINTRAC**: independent, arm's length from police; reports · analysis · compliance · awareness; + **sanctions evasion**.
- **2019 MOU** (first in Canada). PCMLTFA breach can = **conduct unbecoming** (personal account case: $7,500 + **270 days** + ethics course).

### 4 obligations
**1 Compliance program** (5): compliance officer · written policies (senior officer approves) · documented risk assessment · written training · **review every 2 yrs**. Everyone follows it.

**2 KYC** — 6 triggers: suspicious · **large cash ≥ $10K** (single or **24 h**) · **large virtual currency ≥ $10K** · **receipt of funds** (identify your own client) · information records · **unrepresented parties**.

| ID method | Rule |
|---|---|
| Gov photo ID | authentic, valid, current; remote only with authenticating software; **no video call / photocopy** |
| Credit file | Canadian, **≥ 3 yrs** |
| Dual process | **2 of**: name+address · name+DOB · name+account |

- Entities: incorporation/status records + name, address, **directors**.
- **Third party determination** (purchase/sale + large cash); document.
- **Beneficial owner = 25%+** (trace layers: 90% × 90% = 81%). Obtain + **confirm**. Federal corp + high risk → check **Corporations Canada**, report discrepancies. Can't confirm → verify **CEO** + high-risk measures. Keep **5 yrs**.
- **PEP/HIO**: at start of relationship + on **$100K+** cash/VC.
- **Business relationship** starts at first ID; record **purpose** (DORTS) + **ongoing monitoring** (risk-based); ends **5 yrs** after last ID-requiring transaction.

**3 Reporting**

| Report | Timing |
|---|---|
| Terrorist property | **without delay** + RCMP + CSIS |
| Large cash / VC | **15 calendar days** |
| **STR** | **as soon as practicable**; **no threshold**; incl. attempted |

Suspicion ladder: hunch → **reasonable grounds to suspect (possibility) = FILE** → reasonable grounds to believe (probability).
Facts + context + indicators. **Part G** = why (objective). **No tipping off** (can talk to CO/MB).

Red flags: entities/nominees · no financing · unregulated lenders · cash at closing · over-explaining · worried about reporting · indifferent to costs · price far off market · no inspection · quick resale · buy-back · under-the-table · many purchases · anonymous instruments · rush · unsupported finances · buying for minors · name substitutions · **third-party deposit (not spouse/parent)** · offshore financing · shell entities · lawyer-trust anonymity.

**4 Records** (5 yrs; produce within **30 days**): reports · large cash · large VC · **information records** · **receipt of funds** (incl. parent paying → verify them).

### Penalties
Criminal **$2M and/or 5 yrs**. **AMPs**: **$100K** individual / **$500K** entity per violation; **published**. BC brokerages: **$206,250**; **$282,397.50**.

## LOTA / LOTR
- 61.4% of RE laundering cases used **nominees**.
- Transparency **declaration** for fee simple, life estate, **lease > 10 yrs**, agreement-for-sale rights. **Report** if reporting body (corps, trustees, partners).
- Corp interest holder = **10%+** shares/votes or board control.
- LOTR ≠ title; **doesn't confirm ownership**; 90-day delay; omissions possible.
- **Lawyers/notaries file**, not licensees. Useful for KYC.

## Climate risk
Values · insurability · financing · costs. Review **s.219 covenants** · know local hazards · refer to insurers/MBs/lawyers/engineers · **subjects** · sellers **keep insurance** to completion · protect own business.

## Professionalism & ethics
- Profession: public first · expertise · education · objectivity · conduct rules · accountable + hold others accountable.
- **Professionalism** = skills, judgment, behaviour. **Ethics** = moral principles. **Spirit > letter**.
- **Conduct unbecoming** reaches private life (café bragging; repeat impaired driving). RESA **s.10**, Rules **s.23**.
- **Trust** = most valuable, most fragile. Built on **honesty** (s.33) + **integrity**.
- **Headline test**. Discriminatory instructions → **Human Rights Code**; present **every** offer.

### Sources of duties
Contract · tort (care) · agency/fiduciary (perform personally; loyalty) · **Rules s.30** · **ss.33–34 (clients + non-clients)** · RESA s.35.

### Rules s.30
(a) **best interests** · (b) **lawful** instructions (get in writing) · (c) within **authority** (s.45 written authority to sign) · (d) **independent advice** (GST cases) · (e) **confidentiality — forever** · (f) disclose **material** info · (g) present **all offers** promptly/objectively · (h) **reasonable efforts to discover facts** · (i)–(j) **avoid conflicts; disclose promptly, fully, in writing, separately**.

### Conflicts
Substantial risk from own interest or duty to another. Not automatically wrong. Examples: vacation rush · referral fees · two buyers one house · buyer likes your listing · friend wants deal · commission itself.
**2024 BCSRE 1**: $50K loan to client → **$5,000** + **$22,958.18** + courses.
Manage: anticipate · talk early · reasonable third-party test · **disclosure ≠ cure**. Own warehouse → leave it out, or **end agency** and refer.

### s.34 reasonableness
Competent professional standard. Tests: **reasonable informed third party**; **panel of 5 managing brokers**. Ignorance ≠ excuse.

### Third parties
No fiduciary duty, but **honesty + care + no conduct unbecoming**.

### Disclosure: Early · Continuous · Full ("when in doubt, disclose") · Written.

## Duty to report
Why: boots on ground · consumers don't complain · after the fact · peer pressure.
- **Rules s.29**: reps/ABs → **managing broker**, promptly: professional misconduct · conduct unbecoming · improper/negligent; own, those acting for you, **anyone in brokerage** (incl. other laws).
- Other brokerages: **ethical** duty; start with MB.
- Ask: harm? likely harm? undisclosed benefit? unaware/unwilling? → any yes = report.
- Direct to **BCFSA**: urgent risk · MB unavailable · concerns about MB handling · **MB is the problem** · comfort. **Anonymous tip line** (give enough detail).
- Never threaten to report (**extortion**); retaliation = conduct unbecoming (voicemail case).
- **MB (s.28)**: reasonable steps; options incl. BCFSA, counsel, termination, policies, training, compensation. **Compensation ≠ no report.**
- **REALTOR® Code**: Preamble · Code of Ethics (**Golden Rule**) · **29 articles**; **law prevails**. Codes may inform standard of care.

## Ethical decision framework (5)
1 Situation & issues · 2 Stakeholders · 3 Alternatives · 4 Evaluate · 5 Choose, justify, communicate, report, **document**.
