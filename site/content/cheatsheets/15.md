# Lesson 15 Cheat Sheet — Ch 20–23: Construction and Appraisal

## Codes and regulators
- Codes are **minimums**. NBC ~every **5 yrs** · BCBC **2024** · Vancouver **VBBL**.
- **BC Housing LCS**: builder licences, New Homes Registry (online after **Nov 19, 2007**), owner-builders. **Technical Safety BC**: electrical/gas permits.
- Inspectors licensed by **Consumer Protection BC** · give **3** names · referral fee disclosed in writing (**s.56**) · document a declined inspection.

## Homeowner Protection Act (1998, leaky condos ~$4B)
- Builder licence: **24 months** experience in last **5 yrs** + CPD.
- New home = during construction or **10 yrs** from occupancy permit, **any owner**. Substantial reconstruction: **>75%** above foundation, or new foundation + **50%** new structure.
- **2-5-10**: materials/labour · envelope · structural. Limits ≥ lesser of **$200K**/value (fee simple), **$100K**/value (strata unit).
- Owner-builder: individual · live **1 yr**, no renting · no sale within **12 months** · disclosure notice for sales within **10 yrs** · personally liable.
- Discipline: **$1,200** fine (rebuilt home listed without warranty); buyer's agent **7-day** suspension + **$1,250**.

## Measurement
- Exterior walls · **centre line** of party walls · above grade = **entirely** above ground. m² × **10.76** = sq ft.
- Excluded from finished area: garage, unfinished, open-to-below, no-floor protrusions, ladder-only.
- 2×4 ≈ **38 × 89 mm** · module **100 mm** · ceiling **2,400 mm** · sheets **1,200 × 2,400 mm**.

## Appraisal basics
- Appraisers **exempt** from RESA (RESR **s.2.8**); AIC **CRA**/**AACI**; **CUSPAP**.
- Floor (seller min) · ceiling (buyer max). Market value vs value to owner vs investment value.
- Effective date can be **past**. Values **legal interests**. Disclaimers don't cure negligence.
- **7 steps**: define problem → scope → data → analysis (HBU) → approaches → **reconcile** → report (oral/form/narrative).
- **HBU**: legal · physical · financial · maximally productive. Residual method for development land.
- Error **5–10%** reasonable. Cases: *Hedley Byrne*, *Windsor Motors*, *Baxter v. Gapp*, *Cantwell v. Petersen*, *Bourne and Cox v. Martin*.

## Direct comparison
- **Substitution**. Three-point test: **recent** · **similar** lot/improvements · similar **title/conditions**.
- Comp **better** → **subtract**; comp **worse** → **add**. Adjust comp **toward subject**.
- Fewer adjustments = better · net < ~**20%** · watch **gross** · don't **average** · no price indices · listings weak evidence.
- Below-market **VTB** → adjust comp **down** (cash equivalent). Related-party sales out.
- CMA: **3–5** comps, superior/inferior/similar, plus current listings.

## Cost approach
**Value = site value + replacement cost new − depreciation**

- **Current** + **replacement** cost. Site value by direct comparison.
- Age-life: **effective age ÷ economic life**. Market extraction: (price − land) vs cost new.
- Breakdown: physical / functional (curable or incurable) · **external** = always incurable.
- Best for new and special-purpose (churches), insurance, assessment.

## Income approach
**I = R × V · V = NOI ÷ cap rate**

- GPR (market rent) + other income − vacancy/bad debt = **EGI** − operating expenses = **NOI**.
- Fixed: property tax (**largest**), insurance. Variable: management **3–5%** (always), utilities, repairs, reserves (appliances ~**7 yrs**), wages, etc.
- **Not** in NOI: mortgage payments, income tax, CCA, depreciation. Vacancy & inducements aren't expenses.
- Immediate repairs → **subtract from value**.
- Gross lease (landlord pays) · triple net (tenant pays all but structural) · absolute net (tenant pays all).
- Higher risk → **higher cap rate** → **lower value**. Never capitalize **gross** income.
