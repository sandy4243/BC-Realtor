# Lesson 3 Cheat Sheet — Ch 4: Subdivision and Title Registration

## Who owns BC
| Category | Share | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Provincial Crown | **~94%** | *Land Act*; **Tantalis**; **district lots** |
| Federal Crown | **~1%** | reserves, national parks, defence, harbours |
| Private | **~5%** | fee simple Crown grants |
| Treaty settlement | — | Nisga'a, Tsawwassen, Maa-nulth, Tla'amin |
| Aboriginal title | — | court declaration or agreement |
- **Reserves**: federal; **allotments** to members; **not under LTA**.
- **21 May 2024**: First Nations can hold/register land **in own name** (DRIPA-aligned LTA/PLA changes).
- ***Tsilhqot'in* (2014 SCC)**: **>1,700 km²**. Proof = **regular & exclusive** pre-sovereignty use (+ **continuity** if relying on present use). Infringement needs consultation + compelling objective + fiduciary duty.
- **2025 Haida Title Lands Agreement**: first federal recognition of title **by negotiation**.

## Subdivision
- **LTA s.73**: common law right to subdivide **extinguished**. Exception e.g. **leases < 3 yrs**.
- **Approving officer**: public interest; good faith + factual basis + no discrimination → largely final. May require **road/park dedication** + services.
- Types: traditional (2-D) · air space · strata.

## Strata
- Strata = condominium. **"Townhouse" = no legal meaning.**
- Strata lot (fee simple) + common property (**tenants in common**). Share = **Schedule of Unit Entitlement** (also splits expenses). Each lot assessed separately.
- **Building strata**: lots carved from building; **land = common property**.
- **Conversions**: council/regional board approval considering **rental priority**, **relocation of occupants**, **building life expectancy**; + RTA. Some places = virtual moratorium.
- **Bare land strata**: land divided; **road is common property (owners pay)**; often + **building scheme**; manufactured home parks, recreational, commercial/industrial.
- **Air space strata**: separate stratas (e.g. residential vs commercial); many easements; long charge lists.
- **Mixed use**: one strata, **no air space plan**.

### 3 principles
1. **A strata is a strata** — incl. **duplexes** (*Whiting v. Peters*, 2021 BCCRT).
2. **Self-enforcing** — no strata police; AGM/SGM majority → **CRT (CRTA s.121)** or **BCSC (SPA s.165)**.
3. **Not on reserves** — SPA doesn't apply (exception: leasehold landlords e.g. **Sechelt Indian Band, Musqueam Block F Land Ltd.**, Strata Property Reg. s.12.1). Treaty lands → SPA applies.

### Creation
- **Owner developer** deposits **strata plan** (building plans filed near completion).
- Plan shows: **unique number** (e.g. VR 150) · land boundaries · buildings (not bare land) · lots **consecutively** numbered · area in **m²**.
- Bare land plan labelled **beside approving officer's signature**.
- Filed with: **Schedule of Unit Entitlement** · **Schedule of Voting Rights** (if ≥1 **non-residential** lot) · **non-standard bylaws**.
- → separate **title per strata lot**.
- ✅ BCFSA: search title **+ strata plan + amendments + common property resolutions** (myLTSA).

### Freehold vs leasehold
- Leasehold landlords: **governments, certain First Nations, universities**. Ground lease **≥ 50 yrs** → individual strata lot leases. End: **renew** or **must purchase** tenant's interest.
- **SPA s.68**: boundary = **centre** of wall/floor/ceiling. Bare land = per plan.

## Before Torrens
- **Chain of title**. **Void deeds**: forged · illegal · ***non est factum***.
- ***Nemo dat quod non habet*** → break in chain voids all later transfers (Mike/Dave/Scott…).
- Secure only after **20 yrs**. **Recording systems** didn't cure void deeds → title insurance in those provinces.
- ✅ Government photo ID; **CREA Individual / Corporation-Entity ID Record** (PCMLTFA).

## Torrens — 4 pillars
**Robert Richard Torrens**, South Australia **1858** (ship registry idea). Certificate of title. Guarantees: accurate · only source · indefeasible fee simple.

### 1. Indefeasibility
- Registered fee simple = **conclusive**. Good faith + **valuable consideration** + registration → protected even if seller was fraudster.
- **LTA s.25.1**: void instrument → valid only if good faith **and** valuable consideration. **Gift ✗, $1 ✗, market value ✓.**
- **10 exceptions (LTA s.23)**: (1) Crown grant reservations · (2) federal/provincial taxes · (3) municipal charges · (4) **leases ≤ 3 yrs + actual occupation** · (5) highways, public ROW, watercourses, public easements · (6) expropriation/escheat · (7) caution, caveat, charge, builders lien, judgment, CPL **noted on title** · (8) wrong boundary description · (9) **fraud/forgery by registered owner** · (10) *Forest Act* restrictive condition.
- 🔴 **Charges NOT indefeasible** — **LTA s.26** rebuttable presumption only. Arthur/Bart/Carla. ***Gill v. Bucholtz* (2009 BCCA)**: mortgages discharged; lenders no fund claim; lenders bear fraud cost.

### 2. Effect of registration — LTA s.20(1)
- No interest passes **until registered**, except **against the person making it**.
- Royal Bank (Dec 1, unregistered) vs CIBC (Dec 4, registered) → **CIBC first**; RBC can still sue Alan.

### 3. Abolition of notice — LTA s.29
- Buyer need only look at title (+ exceptions). **Not if party to fraud.**
- Knowledge alone ≠ fraud; **knowledge + dishonest conduct**; key time = **contract formation**.
- Anna/Patrick/Mark → Mark keeps (learned **after** contract). ***Woodwest* (1982 BCSC)** → knew **before**, sought to defeat >3-yr lease = **fraud**.

### 4. Assurance fund (LTSA since **Jan 2005**)
- Claimant proves: (1) lost interest due to another's registration · (2) would have recovered at common law · (3) **can't recover** elsewhere.
- **Last resort** (fraudster dead, absent, judgment-proof); fund can recover from wrongdoer.
- **LTA s.294.6(f)**: no pay for share caused by claimant's **own act/neglect/default** (e.g. failure to register).
- Also covers registrar/LTO errors. ❗**Plan accuracy NOT guaranteed** → no claim for boundary errors.
- LTSA named defendant; may settle without action. Nick/Harold/Mac.

## Trusts & forms
- **LTA s.180**: trustee/personal rep may register; **no trust particulars** on register. Registration of trust **optional**; terms only in filed trust document.
- **Form A** = freehold transfer ("deed"). **CPS not registered.** Lawyer not legally required. Written + signed by vendor.
- Mortgages: short form + **filed standard terms** → ❗get **full mortgage** to check assumability etc.

## Title fraud
- Red flags: **changed contact methods** (esp. abroad) · **urgency / below market / no sign / not online** · **new or scanned ID** (insist on originals) · **power of attorney**.
- Prevent: **verify ID early** (PCMLTFA) · title search + **Land Owner Transparency Registry** · conversational questions · verify instructions via **trusted number** · **client password** in physical file.
- Innocent buyer keeps title → owner's recourse: title insurance, assurance fund, **claims vs licensees/notaries/lawyers**.

## Searching title
- **7 districts** (Kamloops, Nelson, New Westminster, Prince George, Prince Rupert, Victoria, Vancouver) · **3 offices** (New Westminster, Kamloops, Victoria) · LTSA since **Jan 2005**.
- Owner at top; **charges** below.
- **myLTSA** (formerly BC OnLine). Search by **PID (9 digits)** or legal description. Find PID: **eValue BC**, municipal GIS, **tax notice**.
- No search → liability, **lost commission**, **discipline**. ***Chand v. Sabo Bros.* (1979 Alta CA)**. **MLS® requires a search.**
- Search early: caller may not be owner / one of several / executor / **foreclosure** / charges.
- CPS **clause 9 Title**: free & clear **except** Crown grant reservations · registered/pending covenants & ROW **for utilities & public authorities** · listed tenancies · as stated. Others → discharge or buyer approves.
- **2016 BCREC case**: incomplete seller-supplied search; 7 charges missed → **14-day suspension**, course, **$1,500**.
- Reserves → federal registries (**Parcel Abstract Reports**).

| **s.219 covenant** | province/local govt; **positive or negative** |

## Registration
- E-filing since **2012**. Re-search → e-sign Form A + PTT → **pending application in 30–60 min** → funds released.
- **On submission**: deposit → **vendor's money**; commission **earned**; brokerage may pay itself if authorized.
- Final registration **7–14 days** → new title.

## Title insurance
- Common law: owner keeps, buyer loses · Torrens: buyer keeps, owner → fund · Title insurance: owner keeps, buyer → insurer.
- Popular in BC: charges not indefeasible · covers **access, zoning, boundaries** · cheaper than survey.
- ❗**Lender's policy ≠ owner's policy.**

## First Nations registries
- **ILRS** (Indian Act reserves) · **FNLRS** (land code, *FNLMA*) · **SGFNLR** (e.g. Westbank). Ottawa, web-based, **not Torrens**, no standard forms, **Ministerial/Band consents**.
- **Tsawwassen** → provincial LTO (special notations). **Nisga'a** → own Torrens-based system. **Maa-nulth** → mixed.

## Contaminated sites
- MoE **Site Registry** (BC OnLine), *Environmental Management Act*. **Separate from title.** Pricier search. Key for **commercial**.

## Manufactured homes
- Licensee can sell **if estate/interest in land involved**. Def: designed to be **moved**, residential, wheels or not.
- *Manufactured Home Act* registry (**BC Registry Services**). **No title · no indefeasibility · no assurance fund.** Unregistered transfer ineffective vs third parties (**s.7(7)**). Mfrs/sellers/importers must register. Check pre-**2004** homes.
- Registration no. + **2 decals** (lifetime). Certificate shows no., make, model, location, owner, security interests.
- 🔴 **CSA sticker** required to offer for sale (or **silver label**) — **≠ MHR sticker** (ID, front left corner).
- **s.18(1)** no moving unregistered; **s.15** transport permit **30 days**, taxes paid. Fines up to **$2,000**.
- Tax arrears → PPR lien with **top priority**. In park → taxed to home owner.
- **Caution**: **3 months** unless court extends.
- *Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act*. **Pad assignment**: landlord's prior written consent; reply **10 days** or **deemed consent**; arbitrator may order. Benefit = **cost certainty**.
- Financing: security agreement to seller · conditional sale · third-party lender → **PPSA**; **financing statement**; exact **serial no.** (= MHR no. if registered). Affixed → also register in **LTO**.
- Chattel (blocks, wheels off, skirting, hooked up) vs fixture (frame removed, basement foundation). Affixed → **rescission period** applies. Get written agreement.
