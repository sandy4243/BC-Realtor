# Lesson 8 Cheat Sheet — Ch 11: Contracts for Real Estate Transactions

## Writing (*LEA* s.59)
Land contracts unenforceable unless: **1 writing** signed by the **party to be charged** · **2 conduct** (paying/accepting deposit) · **3 reliance** (changed position).
- Not for **leases ≤ 3 yrs**. Not void, just **unenforceable**; restitution/compensation possible.
- Writing binds **only the signer**. Rules **s.43**: written service agreement with **owner** unless waived.
- *Pepper's Produce* (2013): verbal landlord OK on a >3-yr lease → licensee liable for advising subject removal.
- Witness = **19+** and **actually sees** signing. Never witness faxed/emailed signatures.
- *T.L.G. v. K.M.G.*: $110K from parents → **resulting trust**. Document family deals.

## Listing contracts
- Seller ↔ brokerage; **buyer not a party**. Clause 7 = designated agency.
- **Strict compliance**: *Brian Fisher* ($140K; listing wanted VTB, offer all cash) → **no commission**.
- **Search title**: *De Cotiis* (client not owner; *LTA* s.23(2)) · *Armitage* (controlled access highway; $50K deposit back) · *Price v. Malais* ("clear title" but 2 easements) · *Chand* (took seller's word).
- **Measure**: *Bouskill* 172 ft "more or less" = 161 ft → unenforceable. Fenced = "what you see". 7-day suspension (trees/driveway).
- **Verify** mortgages with **lender** (assumability); age/lot/zoning at city hall/BC Assessment.
- *Jung v. Ip*: 15-min look, "move-in condition", termites → licensee **20%** liable; employer too.
- **Privacy** since **2004** (PIPEDA federally); up to **$100K**. Listing cl.11, CPS cl.19.

## Competition Act (Competition Bureau)
Criminal = beyond reasonable doubt · civil = balance of probabilities. Ignorance no defence.

| Section | Covers | Penalty / notes |
|---|---|---|
| **s.45** (criminal) | Competitor conspiracies: fix **commissions/splits**, allocate territories, restrict services, ban rate ads, control entry; **wage-fixing/no-poach** | **14 yrs** and/or fine; no harm or performance needed; **implied** agreement enough |
| **s.52** (criminal) | False/misleading advertising, **material** respect, any medium | Indictable: fine + **14 yrs**; summary: **$200K** / **1 yr**; literal meaning + general impression; puffery OK; due diligence defence |
| **s.90.1** (civil) | Agreements **substantially lessening competition** | Market power needed; lease exclusivity, restrictive covenants |
| **s.74.01** (civil) | Misleading representations, **no intent** | AMPs **$750K** individual / **$10M** corp |
| **s.76** (civil) | **Price maintenance** | e.g. board refusing low-commission listings |
| **s.74.05** (civil) | Selling above advertised price | **Owners' licensees exempt**; **developers' not** |

- Cases: **Checkerboard** (60.79 sq ft over → convicted) · Ontario 1981 (83 acres "industrial" was agricultural → **$5,000**).
- Split **on a particular sale** = fine. Sharing strategy with competitors → inferred agreement.
- CREA: **don't collude · don't discriminate · don't mislead**.

## The CPS
- BCREA + CBA (BC). Use **current** version. Before 1987 = "interim agreement".
- **Cl.16**: seller's **risk to 12:01 a.m. on Completion Date** (reverses *Lysaght v. Edwards*). **Cl.8**: same condition as **viewed**. Fire ≠ frustration.
- Don't strike printed wording without legal advice. **Every party** signs CPS + addenda. Number pages. Type it.
- **Parties**: default = **tenancy in common**; explain, don't advise. Court-ordered sale → buyer change needs new application.
- **Non-Canadian ban**: Jan 1 2023 → **Jan 1 2027**; residential in CMAs/CAs; helping = **$10K**; control = **10%+**; students (5 yrs returns, **244 days**, ≤ **$500K**); work permits (**183 days** left). Vacant land ≠ residential. **Never advise**; separate from **20%** APTT.
- **Property**: civic + legal description; **metes and bounds** + sketch for part-parcels; chattels/fixtures; **inventory schedule** for businesses.

| Price/financing case | Wording | Result |
|---|---|---|
| *Occidental Hotel* | mortgage **and/or** A/S | no contract, no commission |
| *Buyers v. Begg* | "**usual terms**" | too vague; deposit back |
| *Jackson v. Macaulay Nicolls* | assume non-existent mortgage | no contract; deposit back |
| *Arnold Nemetz* | "A/S at 8½% P.I. over 10 yrs" | too vague; no SP, no commission |

## HBRP (*PLA* s.42, Jan 1 2023)
- **3 business days**, **0.25%** ($250 per $100K), **can't be waived**. Starts **business day after** acceptance. No Saturdays/Sundays/holidays.
- Mon Jul 3 → **Thu Jul 6**; Thu Jul 6 → **Tue Jul 11**.
- **Excluded**: leased land/leasehold · **auction** · **court-ordered** · **REDMA** units (7 days).
- Notice: address/PID, buyer's name + signature, each seller's name, date. Registered mail = **sent**; fax/email (read receipt) = **transmitted**.
- Duties: DORTS explains it; **s.57.1** disclosure (buyer side preparing, seller side presenting; BCFSA form or CPS); deposit → 0.25% to seller, rest to buyer, **no release needed**; keep notices **7 yrs**. Breach = **professional misconduct**.

## Deposit & stakeholder
- **Cl.2**: within **24 h**; essential term. **Deposit ≠ consideration**.
- **RESA s.28** stakeholder: release only on **HBRP** · **all-party release** · **into court (s.33)**. Promptly into trust (**s.27**).
- Stopped-payment cheque → still deposit it. NSF → tell **MB**. Hand back only if **no trade**. Buyer's lawyer ≠ stakeholder unless undertakes.
- Interest → **Real Estate Foundation** unless agreed (separate account, **s.29(3)**).
- *Lee v. Skalbania*: $975K, $50K deposit, resold +$31K, seller **kept deposit**. **5–10%** reasonable; *LEA* **s.24** relief only if not genuine pre-estimate **and** unconscionable; judged at signing. "Time is of the essence" in **extensions** too.

## Warranties & merger
- Express · implied (builder; *HPA* **10-yr** for non-builder owner-builder) · **collateral** (hard to prove).
- **Merger** on completion. Exceptions (*Di Cenzo*): fraud · mutual mistake/*error in substantialibus* · contractual condition · collateral warranty meant to survive · innocent misrep = *error in substantialibus* · statutory.
- **Cl.18**: only written warranties (and incorporated PDS) **survive completion**.
- *Bouzane* (half-new roof, no warranty) → claim failed. *Olszewski* (1.5 vs 1.15 acres) → refused before completion, **$100K** back.
- Bonus in CPS = disclosure evidence, but **can't sue** on it (privity).

## Builders liens & Norfolk & Aikens
- Holdback **10%**, **55 days**. Knowledge Base holdback clause for new/renovated.
- Strata from owner developer: **mandatory 7%** (*SPA* s.88; Reg 5.2). Liens, not deficiencies.
- **Norfolk & Aikens (1989)**: **cl.13–14** let registration proceed on **lawyers'/notaries' undertakings**.

## Subjects (conditions precedent)
- Not just time. Unmet → contract **ends**. **Cl.3**: removal by **written notice** from benefiting party **on or before** date; else terminates.
- Fulfil or waive. **Condition subsequent** ends obligations; **covenant** = unconditional promise.
- **True condition precedent** (third party) can't be waived at common law; ***LEA* s.54**: **sole benefit** · performable without it · **before deadline**. Write "**for the sole benefit of the [Buyer/Seller]**".

| Subject type | Effect |
|---|---|
| Wholly subjective | probably **no contract** (offer revocable) |
| Wholly objective | **binding**; neither walks |
| Mixed | usually binding; **reasonable steps** |

- *Wiebe v. Bobsien* (Lambert J. dissent). *Pooni* (2022): "sole discretion" feasibility → no contract. *Davidson v. Merrick*: "suitable" waterfront home → no contract. **Cl.22** option under seal (initial).
- Good faith: *Bhasin* (honesty) · *Wastech* (reasonable discretion) · *Griffin* (**best efforts**) · *Gordon Nelson* ("sole discretion" = **honest efforts**) · *Harris v. Wang* (stopped trying → **$274K**).
- Faking "no financing" breaches **s.33** + **s.34**. Subject-free → explain risks + **document**.
- **7 practices**: conditional words (*Gulston*: "shall" = covenant) · who benefits · objective criteria · can they satisfy it (pre-approval) · deadline (backup offers) · **time clause 24–72 h** · get help.

## Amendments
Confirm talks don't end the contract · fresh consideration or **seal** · **cl.12** time of essence → "all other terms remain the same and **time remains of the essence**" · every party signs.

## Assignments
- **Reg s.8.2** standard terms (seller's written consent; **seller gets profit**), residential **and** commercial; not REDMA developer sales. Missing → **Notice to Seller**. Unrepresented buyer → seller's licensee explains.
- No "**and/or nominee**" (*Dunbar v. Dayson*). VTB credit risk. Likely flip → tell seller.
- **CSAIR**: residential strata **5+** lots; full/partial/subsequent; leases **3+ yrs**; developers report **quarterly**, then cumulative + annual; shared with **CRA**; fees count for PTT.

## Negotiation
- Success = efficiency · satisfaction · no value left. **Distributive** (positions, one issue) vs **integrative** (interests, packages).
- **Prep**: issues · **interests** · priorities · brainstorm · objective standards · **BATNA** · **resistance point** · high aspiration.
- Table: package issues, argue standards, reciprocal concessions, active listening, firm on interests / flexible on positions.
- Dirty tactics: false authority · eleventh-hour · deception · stress · threats → name it, break. **Time** + **anchoring**.

## Risk management
- **Shift**: E&O **$2M** (excludes prior acts, property mgmt) · refer out · several experts · PDS. **Anticipate**: document. **Control**: complaints early.
- **PDS** (BCREA **1992**): Land · Services · Building · General; Yes/No/Do Not Know/Does Not Apply. **Not required by law**; **not a warranty**. **PNDS** since **2025**.
- *Sewell v. Abadian* (2025 BCCA): crossed-out PDS + known unpermitted addition → **$300K** deposit back. *Roberts v. Hutton*, *Nixon v. MacIver*: honest belief → dismissed. *Sproule v. Nicholas*: patched new leak → liable.
- Incorporate PDS (entire agreement clause). Don't advise PDS vs PNDS.
- Environmental: tell seller; avoid "no toxic substances"; recommend assessments. Home inspectors licensed since **Mar 31, 2009**.
