# Lesson 16 Cheat Sheet — Ch 8, 24–26: Financials, Completion, Marketing and Technology

## Business forms

| Form | Entity? | Taxed | Liability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sole proprietorship | No | Owner | Unlimited |
| General partnership | No (own name OK) | Partners | Unlimited, all ordinary-course debts |
| Limited partnership | No | Partners | GP unlimited · LP to capital (if passive) |
| LLP | No | Partners | Own wrongs only (law, notary, accounting) |
| Corporation | **Yes** | Corporation | Shareholders: share cost (watch **personal guarantees**) |

- Partners are **agents** for each other. LP who manages → treated as GP. Corporation **never dies**; shareholders own shares, not assets.
- **PREC**: lower rate, deferral, limited income splitting · licensee **still personally liable** · both licensed + E&O · ads say "**Personal Real Estate Corporation**," never "PREC."
- **REIT**: public units · trust untaxed if CRA rules met · limited liability + liquidity · 40+ in Canada. **Joint venture**: liability/tax follow its legal form.

## Accounting

- **IFRS** (public, fair value allowed) · **ASPE** (most private, cost less depreciation). Split since **2011**.
- Principles: cost · revenue recognition (**accrual**; commission at title transfer or subject removal) · matching · materiality (exception to matching) · objectivity · consistency.
- Fiscal year ≤ **53 weeks**. Individuals = calendar year.
- **Assets = Liabilities + Equity.** Balance sheet = a **date** (snapshot) · income statement = a **period**.
- Book value ≠ market value. Corporate equity = share capital + **retained earnings**.
- Principal **not** an expense; interest is. **Land never depreciated.**
- Straight-line = (cost − salvage) ÷ life. COGS = opening + purchases − closing inventory.
- **Goodwill** = price − market value of net assets.

## CCA (tax)
- CRA allows **CCA** only · declining balance on UCC · Class 1 buildings **4%**.
- **Half-year rule** (50% in year 1) · none in year of sale · optional, no doubling up · **recapture** on sale.
- Non-cash deduction → tax **deferral**, paid later in cheaper dollars.

## Selling a business
- Licence: real estate assets / lease assignment → **yes** · **100%** shares of real-estate company → **yes** · < 100% → **securities** · no real estate → no.
- Package: income statement **12 months** ending ≤ **120 days** before signing · balance sheet · **excluded assets** list. Watch inventory.

## Completion
- Conveyancer = lawyer/notary; **buyer** chooses and pays. Prepares Form A, PTT return, both statements (Form F for strata).
- Title clause exceptions: Crown grant terms · utility/public-authority covenants and rights-of-way · listed tenancies. Add anything else (*JBP v. Li*: deposit back to buyer).
- Title = **condition** → buyer can terminate; commission at risk.
- Completion → possession (**day after**) → adjustment (usually = possession). Early keys: reprimand + **$1,500**.
- Title insurance can replace a survey or cover an encroachment · owner's/lender's policies · duty to defend.
- Seller signs first on **undertakings**; pay out once registration is **pending**.
- Buyer surprises: tax adjustment · interest adjustment · **PST on default insurance** (cash).

## Statements of adjustments

| Item | Buyer | Seller |
|---|---|---|
| Price | Dr | Cr |
| Deposit | Cr | — |
| Commission | — | Dr |
| New lender mortgage | Cr | — |
| VTB / assumed mortgage | Cr | Dr |
| Seller's payout | — | Dr |
| Taxes prepaid by seller | Dr | Cr |
| Taxes unpaid (seller's share) | Cr | Dr |
| Rent collected / security deposits | Cr | Dr |
| PTT | Dr | — |

- Credit = paid by / owed to that party · debit = must pay. Same item on both = opposite sides.
- Buyer pays taxes **from and including** adjustment date · penalty = **seller only** · HOG: gross if either ineligible.
- Deposit held as **stakeholder**; commission taken on completion, excess to conveyancer.
- Listing licensee **15%** liable for missing fraud in statements.

## Marketing
- Business mix: **⅔** repeat/referral · **⅓** new. Needs: information · knowledge/skills · trust.
- Shopping goods. Process: felt need → pre-purchase (≈**3** interviews) → purchase (**≤ 1 week**) → post-purchase (**cognitive dissonance**).
- Undifferentiated · differentiated · **niche** (best for individuals). Refer out work beyond your competence.
- **4 Ps** · promotion mix: personal selling, advertising, sales promotion, PR, publicity (least control).
- **SMART** · budgets: % of sales (backwards), follow competition, **objective/task**.
- **AIDA** · eye starts **10 o'clock**, logo bottom · copy ends with call to action.
- Listing presentation: written (CMA, plan, verifiable stats, testimonials) + in person (dialogue, **all** sellers).

## Advertising rules
- **s.37** personal office · **s.40** brokerage name prominent (logo not enough), team name, brokerage address · **s.41** not misleading · **s.42** owner consent.
- Teams: BCFSA-approved; no "Realty"/"Real Estate" except "Real Estate Team/Group" at end.
- Apr 2025: **$4,000–$6,250** fines for unqualified "#1" claims.
- *Competition Act* s.52 criminal · s.74.01 civil · s.74.05 above advertised price.
- DNCL · **unlicensed assistants can't telemarket**.

## Technology
- Listing licensee responsible for MLS® accuracy. Edit out bins, not power lines. Label virtual staging.
- Drones **250 g–25 kg**: pilot certificate + registration · BC *Privacy Act*.
- Social: brokerage name on **profile** · YouTube title/description · Craigslist/Kijiji every post · no translations.
- Newsletters weekly–quarterly · **BCC**.
- **CASL**: consent (express, no pre-checked boxes; implied ≤ **2 yrs**) · ID · unsubscribe · **$1M / $10M**.
- ***Electronic Transactions Act***: not registrable land documents · listings/CPS OK · email name ≠ signature · keep DocuSign certificate.
- Records: **in BC** (RESA s.25) · print on request (s.91) · **7 years** (s.92).
- Passwords ≥ **8** mixed · backups in **2** places · router = first firewall.
- **PIPA**: identifiable individual · excludes business contact info · purpose + consent · destroy when done.
