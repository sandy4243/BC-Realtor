# Lesson 5 Cheat Sheet — Ch 2 (part 2): Trust, Rules, Discipline, REDMA

## Property management by trading licensees

| Service | Trading | Rental PM |
|---|---|---|

| Prepare & negotiate lease, get tenant signature | ✅ | ✅ |

| Payments to third parties | ❌ | ✅ |
| Move-in inspection | ❌ | ✅ |
| Supervise contractors / manage tenancy | ❌ | ✅ |

## Trust accounts
- **s.26**: interest-bearing trust account(s) at BC savings institution(s).
- Licensee → brokerage **promptly**; brokerage → trust **promptly**. 🔴 **Promptly = immediately** (not after acceptance/subjects).
- Cheques payable to **third parties** may just be delivered.
- Interest → **REFBC**, except **client-requested separate account** (client chooses) and **tenant deposits** (RTA).
- **Withdrawals**: mistake · REFBC interest · **earned remuneration** (s.31 RESA, s.61 Rules) · unclaimed money (s.32) · **paid into court** (s.33) · court order · principal's instructions.
- **s.28 stakeholder**: deposit under an agreement held as **stakeholder, not agent**. Out only if above (a–f) · **both parties agree in writing** · **HBRP** rescission (fee to seller, balance to buyer).
- **Crypto can't be held in trust** → third party, no RESA protection, volatility; warn, recommend legal advice, document.
- 🔴 Laundering: deposit → failed offer → refund from trust = "clean" money. Tell MB.

## Rules — 9 Parts
1 **Definitions** · 2 **Licensing** (incl. admin penalties s.26) · 3 **General responsibilities** (s.28 MB, s.29 reps, **s.30 duties to clients**, s.31–32 limits, **s.33 honesty**, **s.34 reasonable care & skill**) · 4 **Business practices** (names, advertising) · 5 **Principals & parties** (agreements, disclosures, remuneration, dual agency) · 6 **Other licensees & unlicensed** · 7 **Accounts & finances** · 8 **Records** (s.93.1 ad hoc, s.93.2 contingent; s.75.1 periodic activity reports) · 9 **Exemptions** (ss.94–96).
Most discipline = breach of a Rule.

## Misconduct (s.35)
**Professional misconduct**: breach RESA/Reg/**HBRP Reg**/Rules · breach licence condition · **wrongful taking / deceptive dealing** · **incompetence** · not cooperating with investigation · not complying with order · breach undertaking · false/misleading statement in RESA document.
**Conduct unbecoming**: contrary to public's best interests · undermines public confidence · brings industry into disrepute.
Anyone may complain.

## Enforcement tools
- **Undertaking (s.53.1)**: minor/obvious; written; may include costs; **not public**; breach = misconduct.
- **Investigation (s.37)**: with/without complaint; licensees, former licensees, officers/directors/controlling shareholders/partners; inspect/copy/remove records (business hours); answer questions; no withholding/destroying. **s.38** justice's order to seize.
- **Rules s.21**: respond **promptly, in writing, on time**.
- **Notice of discipline hearing (s.40)**: **≥ 21 days**; nature, time/place, may proceed without you.
- **Consent order (s.41)**: licensee's proposal; usable for Special Comp Fund claim, **not civil**.
- **Hearing (s.42)**: written/oral; pre-hearing conference; oath; court reporter; dismiss or sanction.
- **Urgent order (s.45)**: no notice, no hearing; suspend/conditions/cease; reasons + right to demand hearing → rescind or replace. (2017 **rent-to-own** case.)

## Sanctions (s.43) — one or more
1 reprimand · 2 suspend · 3 cancel · 4 conditions · 5 cease/carry out activity · 6 course · 7 prohibit applying · 8 **enforcement expenses** · 9 **discipline penalty ≤ $500K brokerage / $250K other — per contravention** · 10 **return of compensation** (≤ remuneration).
- Freeze funds / hold in trust for receiver (usually with urgent order). **s.59** court-appointed **receiver**.
- Penalty money → **education only**.
- **s.47**: publish all orders; linked in **BCFSA licensee search**; CanLII.
- **Unlicensed persons**: same powers; cease, remedy, costs, **$500K corp/partnership / $250K individual**, return of remuneration.

## Appeals
**Financial Services Tribunal (s.54)**: **written**, within **30 days**; **order stays in effect** pending appeal.

## 10 things about complaints
Confidential · licensee responds · resolve early · closed if insufficient evidence · contest or consent order · consent orders & decisions **public** · hearings **public** (unless in camera) · kept informed · right to attend, lawyer, evidence · no unreasonable privacy invasion.

## Administrative penalties
- ≤ **$100,000** per contravention (+ courses/conditions). Between warning letter and hearing.
- **s.26 categories**: **A** business mgmt, low risk · **B** minor, no harm · **C** substantial duties, higher · **D** minor, **until compliance** · **E** non-cooperation with investigations, **until compliance** · **F** **unlicensed activity**, highest (ranges).
- Notice → **30 days** to pay or request hearing → confirm/cancel; generally published. Money → education.

## Offences (s.118–119)
No licence · not paying into trust · improper trust withdrawal · interfering with investigation · not complying with order · false statement.
**Corp**: $1.25M first / $2.5M subsequent. **Individual**: $1.25M and/or **2 yrs** / $2.5M and/or 2 yrs.

## 💰 Number map
$100K admin · $250K/$500K discipline · $1.25M/$2.5M offence · $200K/$1M Comp Fund · $2M E&O.

## REDMA
- Regulates **developers** marketing **development units** in **development property**, **anywhere if marketed in BC**.
- Before marketing (s.3): preliminary approvals (ss.4–10) · utilities/services & title arrangements · **file disclosure statement** with BCFSA.
- Market under **developer's name**. BCFSA **Policy Statements**.

### Development property
| Type | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Subdivision lots | **5+** (unless each **≥ 64.7 ha**) |
| Bare land strata lots | **5+** |
| Strata lots in building | **5+** |
| **Cooperative interests** | **2+** |
| Time share interests | **5+** |
| **Shared interests in land** | **2+** |
| Leasehold units (residential leasehold complex) | **5+** |
**Exemptions**: single-transaction sale of whole development · **industrial/commercial** zoned & used · **leases ≤ 3 yrs** · Securities Act-compliant · municipal subdivision lots (bylaws met / security posted).

### Units
- **Strata lot**: market only after **strata plan deposited** OR **building permit** (or approving officer preliminary approval). PS 5 & 6 early marketing.
- **Co-op**: share/membership + **right to occupy**; Securities Act-exempt → licensees can sell (fourplex example).
- **Time share**: recurring periodic use; incl. **fractional** (1/5 ≈ 10 weeks).
- **Shared interest**: co-own with others + agreement limiting use to part (1/8 TIC example).
- **Leasehold unit**: incl. **life leases** (seniors).

### Licensee acting for developer
1 verify **disclosure statement filed** · 2 give it **before** agreement + get **written statement** · 3 check **deposit terms** match → else recommend legal advice · 4 REDMA covers out-of-province units marketed in BC.

### Pre-marketing
OK if no impression of a right to buy. Ads: developer name & address · contact for info/disclosure statement · **prominent disclaimer** (not an offering; only after filing).

### Pre-sale risks to explain
Delays/cancellation · value & financing changes · **assignment restrictions/fees** · substitution of materials/finishes. BCFSA "Understanding Pre-Sale Purchases".

### Disclosure statement
- s.14(2) BCFSA form; **all material facts**; no misrepresentation.
- s.15(1) no agreement until delivered, opportunity to read, **written acknowledgement**.
- s.22(3) deemed reliance → **damages** for **material** misrepresentation.
- **Summary of Pre-sale Risks and Buyer Rights** (Policy Statement **14**) in front of cover page for **incomplete** developments; explain & get **initials**.
- Licensee's own duties continue (Rules **s.59** material latent defects).
- **New** statement required: **developer changes** · **receiver/liquidator/trustee** appointed · **BCFSA requires**. New → **new 7-day right**; amendment → none. Consolidated statement allowed (s.15.2).

### Rescission (written notice to developer; **can't be waived**)
| Right | Timing |
|---|---|
| **7-day** | within 7 days after **later** of agreement / written acknowledgement; **any reason** |
| **No disclosure statement** | **any time**; agreement generally **unenforceable** (s.23) |
| **Amendment not delivered** | if **reasonably relevant** and **≤ 1 year after title transfer** |
- Use a **stand-alone receipt**, not a clause in the agreement.
- **HBRP** (PLA): residential, **3 business days**, **0.25%** fee — **doesn't apply to REDMA** sales.
- Refer rescission questions to a lawyer.

### REDMA penalties
Offence: same as RESA ($1.25M/$2.5M, 2 yrs). BCFSA: cease marketing; admin penalty **$500K corp / $250K individual**.
Licensees disciplined for: pay directly from developer · deposits contrary to REDMA · not recommending legal advice on non-compliant deposits · marketing out-of-province units without filed statement · agreement **waiving rescission rights**.
