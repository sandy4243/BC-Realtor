# Lesson 12 Cheat Sheet — Ch 13–14: Mortgage Finance and Interest Math

## Why debt
- Credit = future savings brought forward; interest = price of buying now.
- Investors borrow to: **diversify** · **positive leverage** (borrow 5%, earn 8%) · **inflation hedge** · **free equity**.
- Owner wants use, income, growth. Lender wants **regular return + return of capital + security**.

## Capital and mortgage markets
- Savers → borrowers; **rates balance supply and demand**.
- Mortgage rate = **base return + risk premium + management cost**. Mortgages > bonds (risk, illiquid, management). Longer term → higher rate.
- Lenders usually keep the market rate and adjust **non-rate terms** (term, amortization, LTV).
- **Primary** = new loans; **secondary** = existing loans traded. Mortgage rates are **sticky** (advance commitments 30–60 days).
- Small-investor problems: uniqueness · illiquidity · **reinvestment** · administration · capital cost.
- **NHA MBS** (CMHC, **1986**): insured residential pools; **monthly**; CMHC-approved issuers. **CMBs** (**2001**, Canada Housing Trust): interest **every 6 months**; 10-yr CMB **2008**. **MICs**: shares; **100%** of income passed through; uninsured; 1–3 yr terms.

## History and rules

| When | What |

| Post-WWII | NHA **default insurance** |

## Classifying mortgages
- Property type · insurance · source · **priority** (first = first **registered**).
- **Conventional** ≤ **80%** LTV. **Insured** > 80% (CMHC, Sagen, Canada Guaranty); **borrower pays** ~0.6–4%; protects **lender**.
- Banks largest since **1979**; trusts largest **1967–78** (spread ~**1.5%**); **monolines** via brokers only; **life insurers** → commercial; private (vendors, syndicates, MICs); government.

## Repayment plans

| Plan | Key feature |
|---|---|
| Interest accruing | No payments; all due at end; short |
| Interest only | Principal never falls; development |
| Straight line | Equal principal; **payments decline** |
| Constant payment | Equal blended payments; early = mostly interest |

- **Partially amortized**: amortization > term → balloon at term end. Amortization: insured **25–30**, uninsured **30–35** (20%+ down).
- **Balloon** = any principal beyond regular payments.
- **Accelerated biweekly** = ½ monthly × 26 = **13** monthly payments/yr.
- **Open** (prepay anytime, higher rate, short) · **closed** (penalty) · **convertible**.
- **VRM** = prime ± x; usually cheaper; borrower bears rate risk. Fixed tracks **bond yields**; terms to 10 yrs.
- **RAM**: lender pays borrower. **GPM**: rising payments. **Combination**: fixed then variable. **Hybrid**: split. **HELOC**: ≤ **65%** − mortgages; not insured. **Construction**: staged, interest only. **Participation/SAM**: lender shares income/appreciation. **Improvement**: 5–10% down, ≤ 4 units.

## Interest Act basics
- s.6: blended payments → rate stated **yearly or half-yearly, not in advance** → Canada quotes **j₂**.
- s.10: **individual**, term **> 5 yrs**, after **5 yrs** → pay off + **3 months' interest**.

## Rates
- **jₘ = i × m**; **i = jₘ ÷ m**; **j₁** = effective annual rate.
- Equivalent rates = **same j₁**. Compare by converting to j₁.
- Conversion: rate ■ NOM% · m ■ P/YR · ■ EFF% · new m ■ P/YR · ■ NOM%.
- j₂ 5% = j₁ 5.0625% = **j₁₂ 4.948699%**. j₁₂ 6% = **j₂ 6.075502%**.
- *Haptom v. Pahl*: "24% per annum" + $3,000/month interest-only → read as 24% compounded **annually**; overpayment credited.

## HP 10bII+
- **Orange** shift; ■ DISP 6; **End** mode (no BEG); ■ C ALL clears; RCL checks.
- Borrower: **PV +, PMT −, FV −** (lender reverse). Use **+/−**.
- **N, I/YR, PMT same frequency**. Payment frequency drives; else compounding drives.
- I/YR = **nominal** %, P/YR = frequency. **Never re-key** a converted rate.
- Lump sum: FV = PV(1+i)ⁿ; PMT = 0.

## Constant payment mortgages
- Four pieces: **PV, rate, N, PMT** (FV 0 if fully amortized).
- Payment: $500K, j₂ 5%, 25 yr → **$2,908.02**.
- OSB: payment over amortization → **re-enter rounded PMT** → N **INPUT ■ AMORT** → = = = (principal, interest, **balance**).
- Year 2 = **13 INPUT 24 ■ AMORT**; whole term = 1 INPUT 36.
- "no Solution" for N = payment too small.
- Rounded up → smaller final payment.
- Higher rate → smaller loan for the same payment; longer amortization → smaller payment, more interest (diminishing).
