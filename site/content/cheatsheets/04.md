# Lesson 4 Cheat Sheet — Ch 2 (part 1): RESA, Licensing and the Regulator

## History & framework
- **2004/2005**: RESA + REDMA replace *Real Estate Act*.
- **2016**: rule-making → Superintendent; individual max penalty **$10K → $250K (×25)**.
- **2021**: RECBC + OSRE → **BCFSA** (single regulator); BCFSA makes the **Rules**.
- **RESA** (statute) → **Regulation** (Cabinet, s.130; force of law) + **Rules** (BCFSA, s.89.2). bclaws.ca.
- Purpose = **protect the public**: competency to get licensed · conduct standards · regulator.

## RESA s.3 — licence required if ALL three
**Real estate services** + **to/on behalf of another** + **for/in expectation of remuneration** → licensed or exempt.

**Real estate services** = trading · rental property management · strata management.
- **Trading** (8): advise on price · representations · find property · find acquirer · show · negotiate price/terms · present offers · **receive deposits**.
- **Trade in real estate** = purchase/sale/**lease**/other acquisition or disposition · **assignment** of purchase/sale/lease contract · **prospective** trade.
- **Real estate** incl. **co-op, shared interest, time share** (even if not interests in land).
- **Rental PM**: trading re rental · collect rent/security deposits · manage (payments, contracts, supervise, landlord–tenant).
- **Strata mgmt**: collect/hold strata fees & levies · delegated powers (payments, contracts, supervise, **enforce bylaws**).

## Once a licensee, always a licensee
- RESA covers services **for yourself**, **unpaid**, or otherwise **exempt**.
- **s.7**: all services **through your brokerage**; paid **only by brokerage**; ads show brokerage name.
- Own property: **no E&O coverage** for own-interest deals; disclose interest; better to have another licensee list.
- 2019 case: helped overseas friend rent house, paid directly → **$7,500** + **$1,500** + course.
- No independent work for developer / as appraiser / caretaker. Discuss other jobs with managing broker; BCFSA Practice Standards Advisors.

## Exemptions
**RESA s.3**: court authority · bankruptcy trustee/receiver/liquidator · **executor/administrator** · trustee (will/settlement/deed of trust) · trust company (own/held/administered) · **practising lawyer**.

**Regulation — general (4)**: employees of a principal · collection agents · mining/oil & gas · governments.
**Regulation — trading (8)**: **developer employees** · **notaries** · **accountants (business sales)** · **appraisers & inspectors** · **auctioneers** · general info / publishing ads · **referrers** · expropriating authority agents.
- Developer employee: written disclosure (**not licensed, employed by developer, acts for developer**), **promptly, before agreement**, **separate** from agreement & disclosure statement.
**Rental PM**: owners' caretakers/managers · limited brokerage caretakers · BC Housing · lenders/mortgage brokers under **assignment of rents**.
**Strata**: lot owners · caretakers/managers · owner developers.

**Rules**: **ss.94–95** own/family rentals · **s.96** strata council: written disclosure to strata + copy to MB · **no sole signing authority** · **unpaid**.

## Agency & fiduciary
Agent acts for **principal** with third parties. Fiduciary duties: **loyalty** · **avoid conflicts** · **full disclosure** · **confidentiality** · **obey lawful instructions**. Ask: *best interests of my principal?*

## Regulator
- **BCFSA** = Crown agency: real estate licensees, unlicensed activity, development marketing, mortgage brokers, credit unions, pensions, trust & insurance cos, CUDIC.
- **Superintendent** = BCFSA **CEO** (appointed by board); licensing, investigations, discipline; **delegates** (25,000+ licensees).

## REFBC (1985)
- Board of Governors: 3 province + UBCM + BCREA + REIBC + BC Non-Profit Housing Assn; 3-yr terms, max 6 consecutive yrs.
- Gets **trust account interest** EXCEPT client-specific accounts & tenants' security/pet deposits (RTA).
- Grants: land use, built environments, fresh water, food sovereignty, real estate profession.

## E&O Insurance Corporation (1988) — Indemnity Plan (mandatory)
- 7 directors: 1 Cabinet, 3 BCREA, 3 by other four.
- **$2M** per licence **incl. defence costs**; insurer may **settle without consent**.
- **$2,000 deductible per licensee** when damages paid; **first-dollar defence** (no deductible if only defence).
- **$315/yr ($630/2 yrs)**; PREC no separate premium.
- **Claims-made**; claims made & pursued **in Canada**.
- **Exclusions**: insurance agent/builder/developer/mortgagee/notary/appraiser/admin · **own-interest deals** · **commission disputes** · **bodily injury** · **dishonest/fraud/criminal** · **Human Rights Code** · **RTA**.
- 🔴 **No coverage for BCFSA fines/penalties** (page-one title case: $8,500 + $1,250).
- High-value/commercial → **excess E&O**, cyber, CGL, discipline defence, property/business interruption. Related claims share one limit.

## Compensation Fund Corporation
- 3 BCFSA + 2 BCREA directors. **Special Compensation Fund** (licensee fees).
- Covers **misappropriation**, intentional failure to pay/account, **fraud** by licensee/officers/staff/contractors.
- Not for licensees' remuneration. Written claim to BCFSA; time limits; court/discipline findings **binding**.
- Max **$200,000 per claimant**, **$1M per brokerage**. Licensee must **immediately notify** BCFSA & Corp of related proceedings.
- 2019 case: $95,000 compensation; fraud conviction; $172,352 restitution.

## Voluntary organizations
- **CREA**: national; **REALTOR® Code** (1913): Preamble · Code of Ethics (golden rule) · **Standards of Business Practice (29 articles)**. ~99% of licensees. **realtor.ca ≠ MLS®**.
- **BCREA**: **8 boards**, 26,000+ REALTORS®; **standard forms**, newsletters, research, advocacy, **PDP**.
- **8 boards**: Interior · BC Northern · Chilliwack · Fraser Valley · Powell River Sunshine Coast · Greater Vancouver · Vancouver Island · Victoria. Societies; run **MLS®**; arbitrate member disputes (commissions). **Not required for licence; required for MLS® & REALTOR® mark.**
- **REIBC**: RI designation; 1 REFBC governor. **REIC**: FRI, **CLO**, CPM, ARM, **ACoM**, CRP.
- Others: AIC, **BOMA**, CHBA, PAMA, **UDI**, IREM, CMBA-BC, Mortgage Professionals Canada.

## Levels of licence

| Level | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Brokerage** | only level for partnerships/corps (except PREC); services only through its licensees; **contracts with brokerage**; needs MB; **sound finances** (s.12 Rules); records **in BC, 7 yrs**; annual filing **within 120 days** (FS, accountant's report, activity report) |
| **Managing broker** | acts for brokerage; control & conduct; supervision; up to **4 affiliated** brokerages |
| **Associate broker** | MB-qualified, works under MB |
| **Representative** | most licensees |

- **s.7**: one brokerage only. **s.7.1**: training/supervision only by brokerage licensees. **Vicarious liability**.
- **MB duties (s.28)**: active management & supervision · deal with misconduct · trust accounts & records · **immediate written notice** if deposit not received/bounced.
- **Rep duties (s.29)**: give records · keep MB informed (deposit not received → immediately) · respond promptly · supervise staff · **report misconduct** (own & others in brokerage). Anonymous **tipline**.

## PREC
One director = one voting shareholder = one president = controlling individual. Tax planning. **No liability shield**; discipline flows both ways.

## Teams (Rules)
- Must register if: present as **single entity** · regularly **designated agents of same client** · regularly act like **implied agents** of same party.
- MB approves joining/registering. **One team**, **same brokerage**. Unlicensed can't be members (application rejected); ads must identify them as unlicensed.
- Ads: team name + brokerage name. **Trading only** (no PM/strata via team).
- Whole team = designated agent → **can't split buyer/seller**; all names on contracts (not team name); any member can sign.

## Categories (7)
T · R · S · T/R · T/S · R/S · T/R/S. **MB's category caps the brokerage.**

## Qualifications (s.10)
- Good reputation & suitable · **19+** · education & experience · partners/directors good reputation · no refusal/discipline/conviction showing unfitness.
- **Criminal record check ≤ 90 days** (or Triton Canada). Disclose fully; **qualification hearing** if doubt. (2020 case: honest disclosure + rehabilitation → licensed with supervision.)
- **Education**: licensing course + exam; **Applied Practice Course** (Residential or **Commercial**, ~5 months); **temporary licence** after 2–3 weeks; **CELPIP 7** (or English-language degree / prior Canadian licence). Brokers: + **Broker's Licensing Course**.
- **Experience**: reps none; brokers **2 of last 5 years** (waivable; urgent local need for MB).

## Term & status
- **2 years**. Renew **≥ 30 days** before expiry (continues until decision). **Mandatory CE** first.
- Refusal: hearing opportunity, written reasons, appeal to **Financial Services Tribunal**. Confusing names refused.
- **Inoperative**: leave brokerage → inoperative. **MB inoperative/suspended/cancelled → brokerage → everyone.**
- Voluntary surrender (Rules s.17).

## Rules s.23 — notify BCFSA (and MB) in writing promptly
Discipline/regulatory proceedings (RE, insurance, securities, MBs, accountants, notaries, lawyers) · judgments (RE, insurance/mortgages/securities, fraud/breach of trust) · bankruptcy/insolvency · same for businesses (owned or officer in last **2 yrs**) · **charged or convicted** (not minor traffic).
- 2015: charge not reported → reprimand + $1,500. 2024: MVA charges → $5,000.

## Names, ads, offices
- Ads show licensee name + **brokerage name prominently** (ss.39–40).
- Employee vs contractor: **same duties**.
- Personal office (s.37): **no sign** · **don't answer in brokerage name** · **no office address** on ads/records.
