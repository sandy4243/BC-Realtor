# Lesson 9 Cheat Sheet — Ch 12: The Law of Agency

## Basics
- **Agent** acts for **principal** with third parties; contract is principal ↔ third party. Agent gives **warranty of authority**.
- BC listing/buyer agreements **don't** authorize signing: need **separate written** authority (**s.45**).
- Big three fiduciary duties: **loyalty · confidentiality · avoid conflicts**.

## History

| Era | Model |
|---|---|
| Pre-**1994** | **Seller sub-agency** (buyer's licensee worked for seller) |
| **1994** | **Assumed buyer agency** (still brokerage agency → limited dual agency) |
| **June 2012** | **Designated agency** |
| **2018** | **Dual agency banned** (IAG report 2016) |

- Designated: licensee(s) = **sole** agents; **ss.31–32** narrow s.30. **s.32**: brokerage **supervises**, keeps **confidences**, **even-handed**.
- **Brokerage agency** = only model for rental/strata-management-only brokerages.

## Dual agency
- Conflicting: buyer/seller · lessor/lessee · assignor/assignee · **two buyers same property**.
- Different designated agents, same brokerage ≠ dual agency.
- Unrepresented buyer wants listing agent: other designated agent · **no agency** (DORTS + s.55) · other brokerage.
- **s.64 exception**: **remote + under-served + impracticable** ("not capable of being done"). **MB signs** disclosure form · written dual agency agreements · copies to **BCFSA**.

## Forms

| Type | Examples |

## Who can be an agent
Anyone of sound mind, **even a minor** (*Farajollahi*: 14-year-old). Licensee: **19+**, good reputation.

## Creating agency
1. **Express**: MLC cl.1 + **cl.7** (designated); Buyer's contract cl.2. **s.43** written for **sellers** (unless waived): parties · property · term · services · remuneration (+ co-op share) · privacy. **s.44** copy **immediately**.
2. **Implied** (conduct): advising/negotiating for unrepresented party or leaking confidences → **s.63** breach.
3. **Estoppel**: principal's **representation** · **reliance** · **change of position**.
4. **Ratification**: binds from the start; never rely on it.

| Listing | Commission |
|---|---|
| **Exclusive right to sell** | any sale during term, **even owner's own buyer** |
| Multiple | as above + MLS® co-op |
| **Open** | only **effective cause**; owner's sale = none |

## Authority
- **Actual**: express · implied (open house) · usual/customary. **Apparent** (estoppel).
- Exceeding: liable to principal (contract) or third party (**warranty of authority**).
- Signing: **s.45 written** authority → sign **own name "as agent for"**. Oral instructions → reprimand + course + **$1,250**. Copied signature → **7 days** + **$750**.

## Termination
**Express** · **conduct** (sale) · **operation of law** (death, incapacity, bankruptcy). Early cancel may = **breach**.
**MLC s.14**: expiry · mutual written · completed sale · brokerage licence suspended/cancelled · brokerage bankruptcy · conflict (s.65). Then: remove MLS®, stop marketing, signs, return docs. Notify third parties.

## Third parties
Agent personally liable: **undisclosed agency** · **no authority** · **own torts** ("client told me" no defence). Principal liable too (Simran/Frankie; Isley cockroaches → recovers from agent).

## Duties to principal
- **Contract**: MLC **s.8** (only seller · info to co-ops · reasonable commercial efforts · tell buyers · s.30 · lawful instructions · care).
- **Tort**: fair market value; best offer; decline beyond expertise (s.30(d), s.33, s.34).
- **Personal performance** (no delegation without consent).
- **Fiduciary**: burden on **agent**; **confidentiality forever**.

### Conflicts
- *Galambos*: **substantial risk** of **material and adverse** effect. Disclose + consent, or stop. Anticipate · talk early · disclose promptly.
- Remuneration (Zdeno/Zeus $495K vs open house): honest assessment, client decides.
- **s.65** current clients: **stop for both** (no unrepresented help) **or** keep one with **Agreement Regarding Conflict of Interest** signed **when it arises**.

| Continuing Client | Released Client |
|---|---|
| full duties; can't get Released's secrets | no advice; **confidentiality kept** |

- Own listing to buyer client: **show + general facts** with disclosure; offer/advice → s.65.
- Former clients: how long ago · transaction-specific? · connected?
- Voluntary (buy client's property, referral fees): **s.53**, **s.56**.

### Disclosure, confidentiality, instructions
- **Conduit not filter**. Secret profits forfeited.
- *Baillie v. Charman*: $270K to friend → resold **$317K** → both commissions + **$47K**. *Ocean City*: whole commission lost. 2021: **$7,500** secret payment → **14 days** + $5,000 + $1,500 + course (s.56(2)).
- Confidentiality: MLC **7B** (even colleagues); **4B** publishes listing/PDS/price. Expired-listing email → 7 days + $1,000 + course. **s.59** latent defects override → stop acting.
- Lawful only: no law breach, misrepresentation, or **discrimination** (Human Rights Code).
- **s.33** honesty to all; **s.34** care (2,097 vs 1,672 sq ft → reprimand + course + $1,250).

## Principal's duties
1. Comply (early cancel → damages).
2. **Pay**: RESA **s.4** (no suit unless licensed). MLC triggers: CPS **in term** · buyer introduced in term → within **60 days** or **effective cause** anytime · **ready, willing and able** at list price/terms (even if refused). **None** on HBRP rescission. Brokerage entitled; **quantum meruit**; BCFSA penalty ≤ remuneration; RESA **s.7** pay only from own brokerage.
3. Indemnity **waived** (presumed).

| Rule | Point |
|---|---|
| s.43(4) | remuneration + co-op share in seller agreement |
| **s.60** | **net listings banned** ($375K case: $5,000 + reprimand + course + $1,250) |
| s.66 | don't pay unlicensed; **out-of-province licensee OK** |
| ss.61–62 | paid via brokerage accounts |

## Disclosures
Sole agency · no agency (honesty + care only) · dual (exception).

| Rule | Form | When |
|---|---|---|
| **s.53** | Interest in Trade | before any agreement; you/**associate** (spouse · trust/estate · **5%+** corp) |
| **s.54** | **DORTS** | before trading services; **not** for open house/general facts if no motivation/finances/needs; **listing presentations need it**; keep (s.83) |
| **s.55** | Risks to Unrepresented Parties | helping unrepresented while representing other side |
| ss.54–55 | Residential Tenancies form | landlord's licensee + unrepresented tenant |
| **s.56** | Remuneration (optional form) | promptly; splits + **referral fees**; source + amount |
| **s.57** | Expected Remuneration | **presenting each offer**; **dollars**: gross/co-op/net/other |
| s.57.1 | HBRP | preparing/presenting |
| s.59 | latent defects | before agreement, writing, separate |
| s.64 / s.65 | dual agency / conflict **agreement** | exception / when conflict arises |
| Reg 8.2 | Assignment Notice | offer lacks standard terms |

- **s.56**: Matthew/Jethro **$100** → disclose when recommending + honest opinion. Developer bonus: potential → **s.30(f)**; earned later → **s.56** to each client.
- **s.57 math**: $745K at 7%/3.5% → gross **$29,575**; co-op 3%/1.5% → **$12,675**; net **$16,900**; +$3,000 kickback **$19,900**; double-end **$29,575**.
- **s.53**: rental exemption (personal, **≤ 1 yr**, no option/ROFR); 4 signatures (licensee, witness, brokerage, recipient). *Compar v. Foss* → **voidable**. Cases: 90 days; after-signing disclosure; shadow flip (45 days + $5,000 + $3,000 + 1 yr supervision; max now **$250K**). **E&O excludes** ownership-interest claims.
