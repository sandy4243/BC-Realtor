# Lesson 2 Cheat Sheet — Preface + Ch 3: Estates and Interests in Land

## Land ownership
- **Crown** holds absolute title. Private people hold **estates** granted out of the Crown's "regal estate" (→ "real estate").
- **Fee simple** = biggest bundle. Limited only by: (1) **expropriation & tax sale**, (2) **land use & building regulation**, (3) **others' common law rights**.
- Owner can: sell all, grant all for a term (e.g. **99-yr lease**), or grant part (easement, licence).
- **Real** property = court could restore the thing itself; **personal** property = damages only.

## First Nations land — 3 categories

| | Holder | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Reserves** (*Indian Act*) | **Federal** govt | Nation has **collective right to use/occupy** |
| **Treaty settlement lands** | Nation, often **fee simple** + governance | **Nisga'a, Tsawwassen, Maa-nulth, Tla'amin** |
| **Aboriginal title** | Nation, **collectively** | Survived sovereignty unless extinguished; by court declaration or agreement; **alienable only to Crown** |

Aboriginal title rights: decide use · enjoy & occupy · possess · economic benefit · proactively use & manage.

## 4 characteristics of real property
1. **Immobility** → local markets, location, **externalities**, development patterns. Urban land supply NOT fixed (farm conversion; **agricultural value = floor**; **zoning** = legal limit; intensification; transport).
2. **Durability** → new supply rarely **>1%/yr** of stock; **physical life vs economic life** (economic ends first).
3. **Indivisibility of services** → land + improvements + off-site work together; but **assessment** & **income tax** split land/buildings.
4. **Divisibility of ownership** → horizontal/vertical, airspace, strata, landlord/tenant, partnerships, shares → **small investors, diversification**.
Value of land = value of the **services** it provides (capitalized).

## Market participants (key points)
- Licensees sell **services**, paid by **commission**; board + provincial association + **CREA**.
- **Notaries**: narrower scope (*Notaries Act*).
- **Surveyors**: cadastral surveys, **encroachments**.
- **BC Assessment** (*Assessment Act*): value + **property class**. ❗**Assessed ≠ market value.**
- Rental property & strata managers: **licensed under RESA** unless exempt.
- **Mortgagor = borrower** (gives mortgage). **Mortgagee = lender.** Vendor take-back = seller lends.
- Mortgage brokers (*Mortgage Brokers Act*) usually act for **borrower**.
- **High-ratio > 80% LTV** → mandatory mortgage insurance (protects **lender**): CMHC, Sagen (Genworth), Canada Guaranty.

## Estates
- *Law and Equity Act*: English law as of **19 Nov 1858** applies.
- **Escheat**: dies **without will and without heirs** → Crown.
- ❗Licensees never give legal advice on estates/interests.
- **Fee simple**: "fee" = inheritable; "simple" = any heirs. Transfer = **Form A**. Fee tail no longer possible.
- **Life estate** (freehold, registered as **charge**):
  - back to grantor = **reversioner**; to third party = **remainderman** (on title as owner).
  - **Estate pur autre vie** = sold life estate; ends on **original life tenant's** death.
  - Life tenant pays: utilities, **taxes**, **mortgage interest**. Remainderman pays: **principal**, **insurance**.
  - All parties sign listing; lenders reluctant (may want life insurance).

| Type | Example | Liable? |
|---|---|---|
| Voluntary | demolish garage | **Yes** |
| Permissive | let roof rot | **Generally no** |
| Ameliorating | add deck | Yes, **usually no damages** |
| Equitable | arson / malicious | **Yes** |

"**Without impeachment for waste**" excuses 3 **common law** types only; equitable must be **expressly** excused.

## Leasehold
- **LTA s.23**: lease **≤ 3 years + actual occupation** binds buyer **even unregistered**.
- Aboriginal title ≠ estate.

## Interests less than estates (run with land if **registered**)
**Easement** — dominant (benefits) / servient (burdened).
- 3 reqs: both tenements exist; **accommodates** dominant land; **capable of grant** (defined, capacity).
- Created: statute · express · **implied** (support, well, necessity) · ~~prescription~~ **abolished in BC**.
- **SRW, LTA s.218**: **no dominant tenement needed** (Hydro, pipelines, sewers).
- Charge on **servient** title; legal notation on **dominant** title.
- End: agreement or **PLA s.35**. Very short-term → may be a licence.

**Restrictive covenant** — covenantor (bound) / covenantee (benefits).
- 5 reqs: **negative** · benefits covenantee's land · both lands **precisely identified** · **intended to bind land** ("heirs and assigns") · **notice** (registration = notice).
- 🔴 **Positive covenants don't run** — BUT *Crystal Square* (**2020 SCC**): strata paid for years → bound by **conduct** (new contract).

**Building scheme** — **LTA s.220(1)** Declaration; **common vendor**; negative · defined lands · registered title. Common in **bare land strata**. Change: **all owners** (+ mortgagee consent). Enforcement can be weak.

**Profit à prendre** — take minerals/timber/fish/gravel; **can be in gross**; no fixed term; **cannot be implied**.

**PLA s.35** court cancel/modify: **obsolete** · impedes reasonable use w/o benefit · covenantee agreed (expressly/by conduct). Don't advise → lawyer.

❗Never say "**clear title**" if charges remain after completion.

**Licence** — permission only. **Not an interest, not registrable, doesn't run.** (1956 pipe example → licence.)

## Above / below / water
- **Airspace**: only as much as you can **effectively use**; *Aeronautics Act*. **Air space parcels** (LTA) ≠ **density/FSR** (zoning).
- **Subsurface** (*Land Act* s.50): gold & silver (orig.) · base minerals **1897** · coal & petroleum **1899** · gas **1951**. Geothermal → province. Beds of water bodies → Crown (*Land Act* s.55).
- **Water Sustainability Act** (**29 Feb 2016**): all surface + ground water vested in province; licence needed (FrontCounter BC) except **domestic groundwater**. **Not in LTO.** Cancelled after **3 consecutive yrs** non-use or non-payment. **Arrears pass with land.** Change of Water Licence Holder form.
- **Support**: natural vertical + lateral for **land only**, not buildings (need easement).

## Fixtures vs chattels — *Stack v. T. Eaton* (1902)
1. Not attached (own weight) → **presumed chattel**.
2. Attached even slightly → **presumed fixture**.
Rebut by **degree** and **object/purpose** of annexation ("**better use**" of the chattel → stays chattel).
- Pictures on wires → chattels. *Monical* (2013 BCSC): troughs & welded Hi-Hog → **chattels**.
- ✅ Walk through, ask both sides, **write it in the contract**. Risk: lost commission.

## Co-ownership

| | Joint tenancy | Tenancy in common |
|---|---|---|
| Survivorship | **Yes** | No |
| Will | **Can't** leave by will | Can |
| Unities | **4: Time, Title, Interest, Possession** | **Possession only** |
| Shares | equal, undivided | can be **unequal** |
| Default (PLA) | must be **express** | **presumed** |

- End JT: **severance** (sale, mortgage, seizure) · partition by agreement · court (*Partition of Property Act*). JT can sever by **transfer to self, no notice**.
- Xavier sells → Yasmin & Zeb JT of 2/3, TIC with buyer.
- **PPA s.6**: ≥ **50%** owner → court **must** order sale unless good reason (*ter Borg v. Morris*, 2012). Minority can also apply.
- ✅ **All co-owners sign.** Recommend written co-ownership agreement. Don't advise which form.

## Torts
- **Trespass**: enter / remain / place thing (incl. air & subsurface). Remedies: damages, injunction, **self-help** (ask first, reasonable force). Excuses: permission, statute, easement.
- **Nuisance**: **substantial + unreasonable** interference. Factors: nature of harm · neighbourhood · sensitivity · conduct · frequency/duration. Loss of amenity (scrutinized) vs material injury (easier). Remedies: damages, injunction, **abatement** (reasonable, no danger, **notice** unless emergency). *Segal v. Derrick Golf* (1977): not trespass, **nuisance** → damages + injunction.
- **Occupiers Liability Act (1974)**: occupier = possession or control; **multiple** possible incl. **licensee at open house**. s.3 duty: reasonable care → reasonably safe. s.3(3) willingly assumed risk → only no intentional harm/reckless disregard (criminal trespassers; unpaid rural/recreational). s.3(4) higher statutory standards prevail (RTA, WCA).
